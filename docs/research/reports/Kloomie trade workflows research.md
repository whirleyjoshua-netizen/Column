# Teach one job engine every trade's rules

Kloomie's 40 trades don't need 40 apps. They run on **five operating loops**: the service call (diagnose, then fix), measure-order-install, the phased project, the recurring route and emergency dispatch. What separates one trade from another sits on top of those loops: the record of the customer's equipment, how the trade measures and prices, which outside parties hold up the job (permit office, HOA, utility, insurer, supplier), and which compliance logs it has to keep. Kloomie's record engine already stores most of the data these trades capture (measure, readings, equipment, materials, permit, signature, sealed secret and location fields, plus visits, routes, quotes, invoices and Flows). It is missing the machinery that takes admin work away. Verified gaps in the code include work that repeats on a schedule, automations that can change a record, options on quotes, deposits with progress billing and change orders, supplier orders, calendar and map views, and Nevada compliance on documents. The research supports building about a dozen shared engine pieces first, in the ranked order below, then family packs, then trade packs. Las Vegas demand points first at HVAC and plumbing memberships, then pool and pest routes, then installers and remodelers who need Nevada contract, lien and tax handling that national tools don't provide. Nevada's core rules are verified in statute text: the $1,000 licensing exemption, the per-license monetary limit, the license number in ads, the Recovery Fund notice, 21-day prompt pay with retention capped at 5%, lien deadlines, the four mandatory waiver forms, and no sales tax line on construction contracts. A Nevada construction lawyer (and a CPA for tax) still has to confirm every legal point before it becomes product text. Most day-to-day workflow detail in this report is **assumed** from industry practice and has to be checked with real operators. The last section lists the questions to ask them.

## How to read the evidence in this report

The brief asked that verified requirements stay separate from assumed ones throughout, so every claim carries one of three labels. **[V]** means verified in a primary or official source (statute, regulator, agency, utility or the vendor's own help pages for that vendor's features), with a link. **[S]** means secondary: a vendor blog, comparison site, directory or news piece, linked but not primary. **[A]** means assumed or inferred: industry convention or the researchers' reasoning, with no source found. An [A] item is a hypothesis to test with operators, not a fact. Platform statements come from `platform_today.md`, which was checked against the code at commit 744c922. They are labelled **[Code]**. Each recommendation says whether it **reuses** a named existing piece, **extends** one, or is **new**. None of the legal material is legal advice.

## Five loops explain how all 40 trades operate

The single most useful finding for architecture is that the six trade families in `server/trades.js` don't line up with how the businesses actually work. One HVAC shop runs three loops at once: a no-cool service call, a change-out that is a permitted project, and a membership that is a recurring route. A pool company runs a weekly route, plus repair jobs, plus an occasional remodel. The repair research found that all nine repair trades share one basic loop of intake, a dispatch with a trip or diagnostic fee, on-site diagnosis, tiered options, approval, fix or order parts and return, invoice and pay on site, a review request, and a maintenance follow-up [S] ([Plyrium](https://www.plyrium.com/academy/hvac/flat-rate-vs-time-and-materials-pricing); [ServiceTitan help](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/build-estimates-close-sales)). The installer research found all nine installer trades share lead, measure, selection, quote, deposit, supplier order, receive, install, punch, final payment and warranty [A, partly S]. Remodel trades share estimate, contract with deposit, permit, phases with inspections, change orders, progress or final billing, punch and warranty [A]. Recurring trades share agreement, generated visits on a route, a visit record with proof, an automatic notice and a batch bill [A, with S support from pool and pest software]. Kloomie's project model already lets one project hold several lists ("types") [Code], so a trade template can ship with more than one loop: a Service calls list, an Installs list, an Agreements list and a customer Equipment list, all linked to the same contact. That is reuse, not new engine work, and it fixes the one-stage-list-per-trade mismatch.

Home inspection is a sixth, smaller pattern: a booked appointment whose product is a report held until the agreement is signed and paid [A]. The non-trade kinds (agencies, UGC, tech teams, ops, equipment and assets) run on time-bound money: retainer burn, usage-rights terms and rental days [S].

## Repair and service trades sell memberships, options and fast dispatch

### What operators actually do

Las Vegas residential HVAC and plumbing is led by multi-trade "air and plumbing" shops and franchises. Every visible leader advertises the same four things: **24/7 or same-day service, a membership club, financing and upfront pricing** [S]. Goettl's "Smart Sadie" plan waives trip, after-hours and holiday fees and gives members priority on emergencies ([Goettl](https://www.goettl.com/location/las-vegas-nevada)). Sierra's S.A.M. plan includes **4 visits a year** ([Lantern listing](https://lantern.llc/b/sierra-air-conditioning-plumbing-las-vegas/book)), which is above the national norm of two. NRS sells a $15/month plan ([nrs.vegas](https://nrs.vegas/plumbing/water-heater-repair-and-installation)). Nationally the field is PE-backed franchisors: Neighborly's Mr. Rooter has 230+ locations and Mr. Electric 220+ [S] ([Franchising.com](https://australia.franchising.com/neighborly); [Entrepreneur 2026](https://www.entrepreneur.com/buying-a-franchise/top-5-maintenance-franchises-on-the-2026-franchise-500)). Service Experts began franchising in 2025 [S] ([franchise.org](https://www.franchise.org/2026/05/service-experts-named-one-of-2026s-top-new-and-emerging-franchises-by-entrepreneur-magazine/)). Kloomie's buyers are the 3–15 person independents competing with these brands. The software's job is to make them look like Goettl: instant booking, on-my-way texts, tiered quotes on a tablet, a financing link and a membership program [A].

Pricing has moved to **flat rate from a pricebook**, with time and materials kept for diagnostics and uncertain scope [S] ([ServiceTitan](https://www.servicetitan.com/blog/flat-rate-vs-hourly)). Diagnostic fees cluster at **$70–$129**, are often credited toward the repair once it is approved, and after-hours work runs 1.5–3x [S] ([Smart Service](https://webflow.smartservice.com/blog/trip-charge-minimum-fee); [Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/how-to-price-plumbing-jobs/)). In ServiceTitan, the tech builds a tiered estimate and taps **Present** to show Good, Better and Best side by side, with member and non-member prices. Memberships can be sold straight from the estimate or invoice, with recurring billing on a card on file [V, vendor help] ([ServiceTitan help](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/build-estimates-close-sales); [proposal templates](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/proposal-templates)). Membership plans typically include **2 tune-ups, a 10–15% repair discount, priority scheduling and $150–$500 a year**, with a renewal target of **85–95%** [S] ([Smart Service](https://www.smartservice.com/blog/how-much-hvac-service-agreements); [ServiceMag](https://servicemag.org/guides/maintenance-agreements-recurring-revenue)).

The pressures specific to Las Vegas are verified. Water from the Las Vegas Valley Water District is **280 ppm, about 16 grains per gallon, "very hard"** [V] ([LVVWD](https://www.lvvwd.com/water-quality/facts)), which drives water heater, softener and fixture work. Landlords reportedly have **48 hours after written notice** to restore AC as an essential service under NRS 118A.380. This comes from news and tenant guides; the statute itself was not read [S] ([KTNV](https://www.ktnv.com/news/investigations/broken-ac-nevada-landlords-must-make-repairs-within-48-hours)). That clock makes property-manager accounts urgent. Clark County allows **one appliance per simple mechanical permit** and offers Saturday inspections for A/C change-outs and water heaters [V] ([Clark County simple permits](https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/government/departments/building___fire_prevention/permit_issuance/building_-_simple_permits.php); [inspections](https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/government/departments/building___fire_prevention/inspection/schedule_an_inspection.php)). HVAC carries the only heavy recurring compliance log in this family. From 1 January 2026 the EPA's AIM Act leak-repair rules cover appliances holding **15 lb or more of HFC** [V] ([EPA ER&R fact sheet](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-01/er-r-fact-sheet-leak-repair-2026-01-13_1.pdf)). The Section 608 leak-rate triggers (10% comfort cooling, repair within 30 days) still apply [V] ([EPA 608](https://www.epa.gov/section608/stationary-refrigeration-leak-repair-requirements)). The EPA paused enforcement of the R-410A new-install ban on 23 Dec 2025 [S] ([ServiceMag](https://servicemag.org/news/epa-r410a-ban-rollback-aim-act)).

Solar is not a service call. It is a long project with outside parties. NV Energy interconnection runs through the **PowerClerk** portal, and the solar rate is assigned when a project reaches **"Inspection Pending"** [S/V-snippet] ([Solar Optimum](https://solaroptimum.com/what-is-nv-energy-interconnection-process/); [NV Energy deck](https://www.nvenergy.com/publish/content/dam/nvenergy/brochures_arch/cleanenergy/Solar-Stakeholder-Meeting.pdf)). Southern Nevada keeps monthly netting, with exports credited at about 75% of retail, but a **daily residential demand charge began April 2026** and is being challenged in court [S] ([Solar Power World](https://www.solarpowerworldonline.com/2025/09/nevada-puc-approves-shift-to-15-minute-netting-new-demand-charges-for-all-customers/); [Vote Solar](https://votesolar.org/?p=10879)). Appliance repair's defining problem is the second trip. Vendors claim first-time fix rates of 68–74% against 85%+ best in class, with missing parts the main cause [S, vendor claims] ([beancount.io](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/08/13/appliance-repair-bookkeeping-truck-stock-warranty-retail-margin-guide)). Locksmiths in Clark County hold a **privileged license** with a background check, and each tech carries an LVMPD work card [V, 2022 packet] ([Clark County packet](https://files.clarkcountynv.gov/Doing%20Business/Divisions/Regulated%20Business/Updated%20Packets/Mobile%20Locksmith%20Packet%20(as%20of%2004.2022).pdf)). City of Las Vegas vehicles must show the business name and license number [V] ([CLV checklist](https://files.lasvegasnevada.gov/business-licensing/Locksmith-Checklist-PL059.pdf)). Nevada licenses these trades under named NSCB classes: C-21 refrigeration and AC, C-1d plumbing, C-1h water heaters, C-2 electrical, C-2g photovoltaics, C-3d overhead doors, C-23 wells and C-27 septic [V] ([NAC 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nac/NAC-624.html)).

## Installers lose their time between the deposit and the install

Every installer trade loses admin time in the same place: **one job becomes several supplier orders that arrive in pieces and have to be matched back to the job before an install can be booked** [A]. Only roofing has strong ordering from supplier to software. Roofr turns a signed proposal into an ABC Supply order with an availability check [V, vendor] ([Roofr × ABC](https://roofr.com/product-blog/introducing-material-ordering-with-your-abc-supply-x-roofr-integration)), and AccuLynx shows order status inside the job [V, vendor] ([AccuLynx](https://acculynx.com/abc-supply-integration/)). Countertops schedule template → fabrication → install with automatic gaps, so an install is never booked before fabrication (Moraware AutoSchedule) [V, vendor] ([Moraware](https://moraware.com/systemize)). Flooring takeoffs from Measure Square produce seam diagrams and keep different dye lots out of the same room [V, vendor] ([Measure Square](https://measuresquare.com/?p=54)). Blinds, windows, cabinets and closets mostly order on each manufacturer's dealer portal and re-key confirmations by hand [A]. Lead times move a lot: Budget Blinds went from about 3 weeks to 12–15 weeks during disruption, and one Renewal by Andersen customer was quoted 16–20 weeks for a door [S] ([Budget Blinds](https://budgetblinds.com/greater-colorado-springs-co/community-connection/blog/budget-blinds-delays-backorders--expected-lead-times); [ConsumerAffairs](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/homeowners/renewal-by-andersen.html?page=6)). Two trips are normal. Renewal by Andersen sends a design consultant to sell, then a separate project technician to measure to within 1/16" [V, older] ([RbA FAQ](https://renewalbyandersen.com/blogs/2016/12/frequently-asked-questions-about-our-window-replacement-process)).

Measurement is captured in four ways: tape per opening, digital takeoff, laser templating, and aerial or photo reports (EagleView, Hover, Roofr) [V for each tool] ([Hover help](https://help.hover.to/articles/1215021-understanding-hover-s-measurements-pdf); [BlinQ](https://www.blinq.com.au/real-time-window-measurements-blinq-software)). Kloomie doesn't need to do takeoffs itself. A per-room, per-opening **measure sheet** whose lines become quote lines and then order lines, with "measured by", "check-measured by" and a lock once ordered, covers the remake-prevention value. Imported EagleView or Hover PDFs plus a few key numbers cover the rest [A].

Las Vegas conditions shape the steps. Tile reroofs need a permit in the City of Las Vegas, though other roof coverings are exempt within limits [V] ([CLV permit guide](https://files.lasvegasnevada.gov/building-safety/WHEN-DO-I-NEED-A-PERMIT.pdf)). Concrete tile dominates, so a "reroof" is usually a lift-and-relay of the underlayment [S] ([RoofersCoffeeShop](https://www.rooferscoffeeshop.com/post/best-way-repair-tile-roof-system-lift-relay)). Clark County requires a fence or wall permit unless exempt, with Type A (over 6 ft or a pool barrier) and Type B (retaining walls over 24") [V] ([Clark County BPG-232](https://files.clarkcountynv.gov/Building%20&%20Fire%20Prevention/How%20To%20Guides/2022/BPG-232%20Fence%20and%20Block%20Wall%20BPG-051%20Final.pdf)). HOA architectural review is a separate step and often stricter [S] ([siteplancreator](https://www.siteplancreator.com/blog/las-vegas-fence-permit-clark-county-requirements-2026)). Solar screens and retractable patio screens are core local product lines [S] ([Bloomin' Blinds Henderson](https://bloominblinds.com/henderson)). The NV Energy Level 2 charger incentive is reported as the lesser of $500 or 75% of cost, but figures conflict across sources [S] ([NV Energy EV handbook](https://www.nvenergy.com/publish/content/dam/nvenergy/brochures_arch/cleanenergy/handbooks/residential-electric-vehicle-charging-station-incentives-programs-handbook.pdf)). Whether monitored alarm work needs a Private Investigators Licensing Board license on top of C-2d is **in conflict** between sources and must be checked [S] ([Skyline Security](https://sites.google.com/a/skylinesecurity.com/compliance-and-licensing-department/state-licensing/nevada-nv)).

## Remodel and building trades run on money that changes mid-job

For a small Nevada GC, money runs as a deposit, milestone draws against a schedule of values, signed change orders priced with markup, retention if any, and final payment after the punch list. Each sub payment is tied to that sub's lien waiver and a current insurance certificate [A, standard practice]. Statute fixes several of these mechanics, so this family needs the most Nevada-specific product. The owner pays **21 days after a pay request** when there is no written schedule, and retention is capped at **5%** [V] ([NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-624.html)). The prime may **stop work on 10 days' written notice** for late payment, no proper withholding notice, or no action on a change order within **30 days** (NRS 624.610) [V]. Higher tiers pay subs within 10 days of being paid (NRS 624.624) [V]. Before signing, a prime must tell the owner about the lien-notice provisions in substantially statutory form (NRS 108.246), and failing to do so is grounds for discipline [V] ([NRS 108](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-108.html)). The NSCB created a B-7 Residential Remodeling class in 2023, and SB 130 added a **B-7 Restricted** license from Oct 1, 2025 [V] ([NSCB B-7 bulletin](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/01-26-23-B-7-Residential-Remodeling-Classification.pdf); [SB 130 flyer](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/SB-130-Flyer.pdf)). Its reported **$7,000 monetary limit** comes from third parties only [S] ([RocketCert](https://rocketcert.com/blog/168-construction/1833-nevada-b-7-residential-license-vs-b-7-restricted-license)).

Leading Las Vegas remodelers market design-build kitchens and baths with B-2 licenses and award badges (Las Vegas Remodel & Construction, Kingdom & Co., Tridel) [S] ([Lantern](https://lantern.llc/b/las-vegas-remodel-construction-henderson/book); [Houzz](https://houzz.com/pro/kingdomandco)). CertaPro franchises lead visible painting search results [S] ([CertaPro Summerlin](https://certapro.com/summerlin/?p=10662)). The software split is clear. Buildertrend and JobTread support priced change orders with digital client approval [S] ([GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/resources/buildertrend-vs-jobtread-for-remodelers/)). Buildertrend reportedly runs **$499–$1,099/month**, with change orders on higher tiers [S, competitor source] ([Projul](https://projul.com/competitors/jobtread-vs-buildertrend)). PaintScout prices painting from production rates per surface, with setup at $999–$1,499 [V, vendor] ([PaintScout](https://www.paintscout.com)). No independent source documented how deep either tool's draw or progress billing goes.

Permits depend on the address, not the mailing city. Unincorporated Clark County uses **Accela Citizen Access**, and North Las Vegas uses a Citizen Self-Service portal that made users re-register in June 2025 [V] ([Clark County Citizen Access](https://webfiles.clarkcountynv.gov/Building%20&%20Fire%20Prevention/IT/Knowledgebase/Citizen%20Access%20General%20Information.pdf); [NLV](https://www.cityofnorthlasvegas.com/business/permit-application-center)). No public contractor API was found, so permit numbers and inspection results get re-keyed [A]. EPA RRP applies to paid work that disturbs paint in pre-1978 homes. It needs a certified firm (5-year certification), a certified renovator on each job, and the Renovate Right pamphlet [V] ([EPA RRP](https://www.epa.gov/node/255903)). The 3-year record retention is [A]. Heat is a scheduling input. Las Vegas is above 90°F from late May to mid-October, so concrete crews pour at 5–6 a.m. and summer schedules include weather-hold days [S] ([Centurion Concrete LV](https://centurionconcretelasvegas.com/concrete-guides/best-time-pour-concrete-las-vegas/)). Handyman work is legally bounded. NRS 624.031(6) exempts repair under **$1,000** in labor plus materials, unless a permit is needed or the work is part of a larger project of $500 or more, and splitting contracts to evade the limit is barred [V] ([NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/NRS-624.html)). Typical handyman rates run $75–$125/hr with 1.5–2.5 hour minimums [S] ([Jobber](https://getjobber.com/academy/handyman/how-to-price-handyman-jobs/)).

## Recurring trades are route businesses with compliance logs

Every recurring trade follows the same pattern: an agreement, generated visits on a route, a visit record with proof, an automatic notice, and a batch bill [A]. Two recurrence models are needed [A]. In the first, a fixed weekday sits on a fixed route (pool weekly, lawn weekly). In the second, the visit falls anywhere in a service window (pest monthly or quarterly, windows quarterly), and the office places it by density. FieldRoutes' emphasis on routing supports the second model [S] ([FieldRoutes](https://fieldroutes.com/blog/pest-control-pesticide-usage-tracking-apps)).

Pool is the most data-heavy visit. Skimmer keeps chemical logs and history **per body of water**, so pool and spa are logged separately [S] ([GetApp](https://www.getapp.com.au/software/113919/skimmer)). It sends a "digital door hanger" recap email with readings, doses and photos [S] ([Skimmer](https://getskimmer.com/blog/best-pool-maintenance-software)). ## Cleaning, restoration, hauling and mobile trades split into dispatch and booked work

Towing is the most regulated trade on billing in the whole set, and its rules are verified from 2026 primary filings. Each Nevada carrier files its own NTA tariff tied to a CPCN number, priced by truck category (A/B up to 15,000 lb, C above) and by day or night. NAC 706.420 requires the bill to **itemize each rate used, with every charge substantiated in writing** [V] ([NTA tariff, Phenomenal Towing](https://nta.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/ntanvgov/content/Carriers/Tariffs/P_to_S/Phenomenal_Towing_7371-tar.pdf)). Storage is billed per 24-hour period by class, with a **police-hold rate of $5/day after day 15** and lien-fee steps at 96 hours, 4 business days and 336 hours [V]. Autura filed for 33 non-consent carriers to raise the pass-through administrative fee from $25.00 to $31.50 [V] ([NTA notice 26-02007](https://nta.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/ntanvgov/content/About/Notices/2026/02-February/26-02007%20Autura_ADA.pdf)). A private-property tow needs the owner's signed request and police notice [S] ([NRS 706.4477](https://nevada.public.law/statutes/nrs_706.4477)). Towbook accepts digital motor-club calls with an ETA and calculates storage [V, vendor] ([Towbook help](https://intercom.help/towbook/en/articles/1337510-digital-dispatching-overview)). Kloomie already gives towing its own stages [Code]. What it lacks is tariff-table pricing, storage accrual and lien clocks.

Restoration is emergency work and depends on documentation. Under IICRC S500 the restorer sets a drying goal from unaffected materials and takes **daily moisture readings at the same points** until the goal is met [S] ([Accuserve](https://www.accuserve.com/blog/setting-drying-targets-utilizing-the-new-s500)). Insurers expect moisture maps, psychrometric logs, equipment logs and time-stamped meter photos. Encircle Hydro builds these offline [V, vendor] ([Encircle](https://getencircle.com/water-damage-restoration)). Getting paid is the main admin cost: thin documentation leads to carrier reductions [A]. Residential cleaning runs on recurring bookings with automated messages (ZenMaid, $39/month plus $14/seat). Commercial janitorial adds geofenced clock-in and scored inspections shared with the client (Swept) [S] ([CleaningOpsPro](https://cleaningopspro.pages.dev/software/compare/zenmaid-vs-swept); [Swept](https://sweptworks.com/janitorial-inspection-software)). Junk removal prices in **eighths of a truck** with an on-site final quote [V, franchisor] ([1-800-GOT-JUNK](https://1800gotjunk.com/index%2Ephp/us_en/how-our-pricing-works)). Nevada intrastate household-goods movers need NTA authority and a filed tariff [V] ([NTA tariff, We Move Las Vegas](https://nta.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/ntanvgov/content/Carriers/Tariffs/T_to/We_Move_Las_Vegas_3369-tar.pdf)).

Among the mobile trades, courier work centers on proof of delivery: photo, signature, barcode and ID [V, vendor] ([Onfleet](https://onfleet.com/proof-of-delivery)). Detailers have the customer sign off on walk-around photos before work begins [V, vendor] ([Urable](https://urable.com/2026/05/15/how-digital-vehicle-inspections-protect-your-detailing-shop-and-boost-profits/)). Mobile groomers assign service areas to days and plan per-van start and end points [V, vendor] ([MoeGo wiki](https://wiki.moego.pet/new-plan-features)). Vaccine-expiry tracking is [A]. Nevada certifies home inspectors as Inspectors of Structures under NAC 645D, in residential, general and master tiers [V] ([NAC 645D](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/NAC/NAC-645D.html)). Spectora writes reports offline [S] ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/157144/Spectora/)).

## The same pieces must work for one truck and for thirty

The growth-stage model in the notes is industry convention [A], but it is a useful design test. A solo operator needs a phone flow with quote, invoice, pay link, calendar, reminders and reviews, and is very price-sensitive. At 2–5 trucks, the first office hire needs dispatch, a tech app, texting, a simple pricebook and QuickBooks sync, and per-user pricing starts to hurt. At 10–30 trucks a business adds a CSR, dispatcher and service manager, and needs memberships, good-better-best with financing, scorecards, job costing and permissions, which is where ServiceTitan wins. Each shared piece should therefore default to the fewest steps and reveal depth only when a team turns it on. Concretely: an agreement should be creatable from a paid invoice in one tap, the way ServiceTitan sells memberships from the invoice [V vendor]. Quote options should start as a single column with "add option" rather than a required tier grid. Deposits should default from the trade template. Readiness, lien clocks and compliance warnings should run without anyone opening a screen, through Flows. Dispatch boards, capacity limits, sub records and scorecards belong to team-size settings. Seat pricing is itself a competitive weapon against Jobber's $29 per extra user [V]. The research did not test Kloomie's own pricing.

## Suggested build order

The order follows dependency first, then Las Vegas demand, then admin removed. **Step 0** needs no engine work: ship trade templates that use what exists today. That means multi-list trade projects (Service calls, Installs, Agreements, Equipment), the credential vault as a list with expiry dates (reminders wait for Step 1's date trigger), sealed entry codes, year-built and HOA fields, and the SNWA watering group. **Step 1** builds the foundation the rest automates through: Flows actions that change records (gap 6), a **date-based trigger** ("N days before or after a record's date field", which expiries, renewals, lien clocks, storage days and rights expiry all depend on; today's `schedule` trigger is fixed weekdays and times only, verified in code), formulas and rollups (gap 2), plus a calendar view (gap 1). **Step 2** builds service agreements and generated visits (gap 3) with skip handling, visit recaps and batch autopay. This serves the Las Vegas HVAC/plumbing membership market and the pool and pest routes, the biggest recurring revenue in the valley [S]. **Step 3** delivers quote options with a pricebook, diagnostic credit and member pricing (gap 7), together with deposits, change orders and progress billing (gap 9). In the same release goes the Nevada compliance profile: license number on documents, monetary-limit and $1,000 warnings, and the construction-versus-retail tax line, after lawyer and CPA review. **Step 4** adds supplier orders and install readiness (gap 8), the measure sheet, the customer equipment record and outside-party gates with dependencies (gap 5). This unlocks the installer and remodel families. **Step 5** delivers the Nevada money-protection toolkit: prompt-pay clocks, lien deadline reminders, the four statutory waivers in locked text, the 3-day cancel notice and texting consent records. It is held until a lawyer has approved the forms. **Step 6** adds trade packs in this order: HVAC/plumbing, pool, pest, solar screens/blinds, roofing, GC, then towing and restoration. Towing and restoration are high-value but narrow, and their specialised competitors (Towbook, Encircle) are entrenched. **Step 7** adds map view, the full dispatch board, offline mode and the photo-intake form view.

## Conclusion

The research points to one structural decision more than any single feature: **model trades as combinations of loops on one shared customer, property and equipment record, rather than as one stage list per trade.** Kloomie's multi-list projects make this possible today. Once that is in place, most of what makes a trade "purpose-built" turns out to be configuration (fields, stages, pricing units, checklists) plus a small number of engine capabilities that every family hits: automations that can act on records, repeating work, options and staged money, gates with dependencies, and orders. Building those twelve-odd pieces well covers more of the 40 trades than any trade-by-trade build.

The second implication is that Nevada compliance is a product opportunity and not only a risk. The national leaders sell the same software in every state. The verified Nevada rules turn directly into warnings, clocks and locked documents that a small operator can't easily produce alone: monetary limits, the $1,000 line, Recovery Fund and lien disclosures, 21-day pay with retention capped at 5%, the four waiver forms, the tax treatment of construction invoices, NTA tow tariffs, and SNHD and NDA logs. The value depends on accuracy, so the lawyer review and the operator interviews above are not optional follow-ups. They are the next step of the build.
