# Cleaning, restoration, hauling and mobile service trades: how they run (Las Vegas first, then national), as of November 2026

Scope note: research done 2026-11-11 with about 20 search and fetch calls. Many vendor pages (Spectora /features, the LVMPD contract news story) would not load. Every claim is marked **VERIFIED** (with a source) or **ASSUMED/INFERRED** (domain knowledge, no source found in this pass). Writers should not upgrade ASSUMED items to fact. The strongest primary-source material is the Nevada towing tariff and NTA notice data (both 2026).

## Towing and roadside (Las Vegas / Nevada): rules, rates, dispatch, software

### Takeaway
Nevada tow rates are not one statewide schedule. Each tow carrier files its own NTA tariff (with a CPCN number) that prices every line item by vehicle category and day or night, and the bill must itemize each rate. Las Vegas law-enforcement (non-consent) tows are coordinated through Autura (formerly Auto Return), which filed for 33 non-consent carriers in 2026 to raise the pass-through administrative fee from $25.00 to $31.50. Towing is the most dispatch-driven trade in this set and the most regulated on billing.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: Tow carriers in Nevada file individual tariffs with the Nevada Transportation Authority (NTA), each tied to a CPCN number. Example: Phenomenal Towing LLC, 2230 W Bonanza Rd, Las Vegas, CPCN #7371, on the NTA "Model Tow Tariff (Flat Rate)" template version 042822, stamped by the NTA Las Vegas office JUL 10 2026. Other templates exist (e.g. "Model Tow Tariff (One-Hour Minimum)"). — [NTA tariff, Phenomenal Towing](https://nta.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/ntanvgov/content/Carriers/Tariffs/P_to_S/Phenomenal_Towing_7371-tar.pdf); [NTA tariff, Milne (one-hour minimum)](https://nta.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/ntanvgov/content/Carriers/Tariffs/M_to_O/MilneRoadServiceLLC_8003-tar.pdf)
- VERIFIED: Billing rule on the tariff: "Tow car operator shall itemize on a tow bill each rate used or fee charged to compute the bill. All charges shall be substantiated in writing and attached to the bill. (NAC 706.420)". Timed services bill in full increments. On multiple tows, timed services not tied to one vehicle are shared equally. The charge runs from departure to the scene until return to the yard. — [NTA tariff, Phenomenal Towing](https://nta.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/ntanvgov/content/Carriers/Tariffs/P_to_S/Phenomenal_Towing_7371-tar.pdf)
- VERIFIED: Vehicle and truck categories on the tariff: Category A/B (tow truck with unladen weight of 15,000 lbs or less) and Category C (more than 15,000 lbs), each priced Day and Night. — same source
- VERIFIED (2026 example rates, Phenomenal Towing, Category A/B day/night): Administrative fee (law-enforcement tows) $31.50. Hookup flat, single vehicle $235.15 / $270.47 (Cat C $302.09 / $341.44). Hookup mileage 10.1–50 mi $11.34 per loaded mile, 50.1+ $8.67. After hours $65.00 (night). Dolly $128.60 / $142.23. Off hook (drop) $132.60 / $157.30. Extra labor skilled $32.50 per 15 min. Recovery (<15,000 lb truck) $54.69 / $69.99 per 15 min. Clean up and stand by, first 30 min free. Lot visit: first daytime visit free, then $65.00. Sit out $56.95. Tape $48.60. Lien fee $149.50 ("either after 96 hours per NRS 706.4468 or after 4 business days per NRS 706.4479"), and another lien fee step "after 336 hours". Oversize is hookup +25%, combination vehicles +50%. — same source
- VERIFIED (storage per 24-hour period, same tariff, day/night columns): as evidence $52.00; unsecured $29.92 / $54.76; secured $53.30 / $91.00; inside $71.50 / $129.35; oversize = storage +25%; **police hold after the 15th day $5.00 per day**; stolen vehicle released within five days of recovery = 50% of storage. — same source
- VERIFIED: NTA Docket 26-02007 (notice dated Feb 5, 2026): "Autura (formerly Auto Return) filed an application ... on behalf of 33 non-consent tow carriers" to raise the "Administrative Fee" from $25.00 to $31.50, "a pass-through expense to the tow bill." Listed carriers include A&A Towing, A&G Towing & Storage, All Points Towing, Anderson Towing, SNAP Towing, Big Valley Towing, City Auto Towing, Commerce Auto Towing, D&S Tow, Desert Towing, Ewing Bros, FAST Towing, Phenomenal Towing, Quality Towing, Silver State Towing, South Strip Towing, Sunrise Towing, The Tow Truck Company, Two Star Towing and others (some are northern Nevada, e.g. Carson City Towing). NTA office: 3300 W. Sahara Ave, Suite 200, Las Vegas. — [NTA notice 26-02007](https://nta.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/ntanvgov/content/About/Notices/2026/02-February/26-02007%20Autura_ADA.pdf)
- VERIFIED (weak; snippet only): a news item reports LVMPD has a towing contract with Autura, and a commenter raised consumer-complaint concerns. The article itself returned HTTP 403, so the contract terms are unverified. — [News From The States](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/node/435779)
- VERIFIED (secondary summary of statute): For a private-property (non-consent) tow, the requester must be the property owner or an authorized agent and must sign a specific request for the tow (NRS 706.4477, NAC 706.4275). A sign must be in plain view saying parking is prohibited or restricted and showing the police or sheriff's phone number (NRS 487.038). Oral notice to police or the sheriff is required unless notice was already given under NRS 706.4477. A vehicle may not be towed solely because its registration has expired (AB301, 2021). — [NRS 706.4477](https://nevada.public.law/statutes/nrs_706.4477); [NAC 706.4275](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/nevada/NAC-706-4275); [NRS 487.038 (FindLaw)](https://codes.findlaw.com/nv/title-43-public-safety-vehicles-watercraft/nv-rev-st-487-038/); [AB301 (2021)](https://archive.leg.state.nv.us/Session/81st2021/Bills/AB/AB301_EN.pdf)
- VERIFIED (law-firm secondary, not checked against statute): a single-family driveway tow needs no sign but still needs police notice. — [Shouse Law](https://www.shouselaw.com/?p=5363)
- Conflict: consumer sites put Nevada storage at $15–$50 per day ([Rental Awareness](https://rentalawareness.com/towing-laws-in-nevada/)) or $20–$60 per day ([TowingCosts](https://towingcosts.com/impound-fees/nevada)). Accepting a digital call with an ETA adds the job automatically. Towbook's features page also lists Honk, which conflicts with the help center. The mobile app tracks and releases impounds and can text or email the impound invoice. A recent release added "Chat with Motorclub". — [Towbook help: digital dispatching](https://intercom.help/towbook/en/articles/1337510-digital-dispatching-overview); [Towbook features](https://towbook.com/features); [Towbook App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/towbook/id670258292)
- VERIFIED (third-party): Agero's Swoop platform feeds jobs to tow software through a provider API. ### Inferences
- INFERRED: A Nevada tow job record must carry the tariff line items (category, day/night, each timed increment) and auto-compute storage by 24-hour period with storage class (unsecured, secured, inside, evidence, police hold after day 15), because NAC 706.420 requires an itemized, substantiated bill.
- INFERRED: Lien clocks (96 hours, 4 business days, 336 hours) should be timers on each impound that prompt the lien-fee step and the owner/lienholder notice letters.
- ASSUMED: Release paperwork typically means owner ID, proof of ownership (title or registration), proof of insurance, a police release for holds, and payment. This was not verified for LVMPD specifically.
- ASSUMED: Day-to-day work is a dispatcher taking motor club (Agero, Honk, AAA), police rotation (via Autura) and cash calls, assigning the nearest truck by GPS, the driver taking photos at hookup and drop, and yard staff running impound releases and lien sales. Roles: owner, dispatcher, drivers/operators (light and heavy duty), yard/impound clerk, billing (motor club reconciliation).
- ASSUMED: The biggest bottleneck is motor-club reconciliation: matching each club's PO and authorized amounts against what was actually done, plus goa (gone on arrival) and extra-mile disputes.

## Cleaning and janitorial (residential maid service and commercial janitorial)

### Takeaway
Residential cleaning runs on recurring bookings (weekly, biweekly, monthly) with online booking forms and automated client messages (ZenMaid, Launch27/BookingKoala). Commercial janitorial runs on contracts with checklists, geofenced clock-in, and scored quality inspections that are shared with the client (Swept). Key and alarm code storage was not confirmed for either vendor.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED (third-party comparison): ZenMaid focuses on residential booking forms, automated client communications and residential workflows. It has "no commercial inspection tools, no multilingual support, and no geofencing". Pro plan is $39/month plus $14 per seat. — [CleaningOpsPro ZenMaid vs Swept](https://cleaningopspro.pages.dev/software/compare/zenmaid-vs-swept)
- VERIFIED (third-party): Swept's Optimize tier, about $150/month for 16–25 locations, adds inspections, geofencing, mandatory break compliance and in-app messaging. Managers run remote inspections with scored checklists, and reports can be shared with clients through Swept's client portal. — [CleaningOpsPro](https://cleaningopspro.pages.dev/software/compare/zenmaid-vs-swept); [Swept janitorial inspection software](https://sweptworks.com/janitorial-inspection-software)
- VERIFIED (vendor): Swept's 2026 update refreshed the Inspections page for cleaning QC in the field. Swept claims "36,000+ cleaners". — [Swept 2026 updates](https://learn.sweptworks.com/swept-software-updates-2026); [Swept features](https://sweptworks.com/features)
- VERIFIED (listing): Swept includes customizable checklists, cleaning instructions and inspections as quality-control tools. — [GetApp Swept](https://www.getapp.com/operations-management-software/a/swept/)

### Inferences
- ASSUMED: Residential lifecycle: online quote (by bedrooms, bathrooms and square feet, plus extras like oven, fridge, windows; or deep, move-in/out and standard tiers) → booking → assigned team of 2–3 → reminder 24–48 hours ahead → team arrives with entry instructions (key, lockbox, garage or alarm code, pets) → room-by-room checklist → photos of finished rooms or problems → card charged on completion → review request → recurring schedule continues. Roles: owner/office manager, schedulers, team leads, cleaners. Terms: "deep clean", "maintenance clean", "move-out", "recurring", "lockout", "re-clean guarantee".
- ASSUMED: Commercial janitorial lifecycle: walkthrough → bid by square feet and frequency → contract (monthly fixed price) → nightly crews at multiple sites → geofenced clock-in → checklist per area → periodic inspections scored and sent to the client → monthly invoice → supply restock. Compliance: business license, general liability, janitorial bonding (often requested by clients), workers' comp, and OSHA hazard communication for chemicals.
- INFERRED: Key and alarm codes are secrets. In Kloomie they should use the existing sealed-field pattern (sealed on the server, visible only on "Show", with every look logged), as the equipment "Locked" field already does.
- ASSUMED: Bottlenecks are rescheduling and cancellation churn, team swaps when a cleaner calls out, re-clean complaints without photo evidence, and payroll from hours across many sites. Automation removes reminders, auto-rebooking, photo-backed QC and payroll exports.

## Water, fire and mold restoration

### Takeaway
Restoration is emergency-dispatched (24/7 water losses) and documentation-heavy. IICRC S500 drives drying goals set from unaffected materials and daily moisture readings at the same points until dry. Insurance claims require moisture maps, psychrometric logs, equipment logs and time-stamped photos to justify Xactimate line items. Encircle (Hydro), DASH/Cotality, Albi and PSA are the leading platforms.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED (secondary): Under S500 the restorer sets a drying goal, usually from unaffected materials in the same building, then measures moisture content daily in the same locations until materials reach that goal. — [Accuserve: drying targets under the new S500](https://www.accuserve.com/blog/setting-drying-targets-utilizing-the-new-s500)
- VERIFIED (vendor blog, March 2026): Ongoing S500 documentation includes daily moisture readings, equipment deployment records, psychrometric data logs and progress photographs. — [CloudPano 3D Measure blog](https://3dmeasure.cloudpano.com/blog/iicrc-s500-documentation-requirements)
- VERIFIED (study guide, secondary): Drying completion is determined by quantitative measurement with calibrated meters against established dry standards. — [Open Exam Prep WRT](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/iicrc-wrt/project-administration-verification/drying-verification-completion)
- VERIFIED: The current US edition is cited as ANSI/IICRC S500-2021. AS-IICRC S500:2025 is an Australian adaptation published April 2025, not a new US edition. — [R&R mag](https://www.randrmagonline.com/articles/91304-australia-standard-published-as-iicrc-s500-standard-for-professional-water-damage-restoration); [BSM mag 2025](https://bsmmag.com/Articles/2025/Daily-Articles/04/Australia-Body-Adopts-IICRC-Standard-for-Restoration.html)
- VERIFIED (vendor and App Store): Encircle Hydro logs moisture, equipment and psychrometric readings and builds moisture maps. It reads meter values from a photo, works offline, has an equipment calculator tied to S500 (air movers and dehumidifiers per chamber), and produces reports with maps, psychrometrics and time-stamped meter photos. Xactimate integration status conflicts: the FAQ says floor-plan-to-sketch is still in progress after a 2023 Verisk announcement, while the App Store says data goes straight to Xactimate for a sketch. Encircle Contents added QR "Scan-to-Track" and an Item Inventory Manager (2026-08). — [Encircle water damage](https://getencircle.com/water-damage-restoration); [Encircle App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/encircle/id604527488); [Encircle estimators](https://getencircle.com/restoration-contractor-estimators)
- VERIFIED (vendor and listings): Albi is a restoration CRM with job and communication history, referral-source and relationship tracking and custom fields. Listings also mention contactless equipment tracking, budgeting, work orders, e-signatures, workflows, QuickBooks/Zapier and an API. Price is cited at $60/user/month (listings disagree). Reviews are mixed, including a payment dispute. — [Albi CRM](https://albiware.com/features/customer-management/crm); [TrustRadius Albi](https://www.trustradius.com/products/albiware/details); [Capterra Albi reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/215149/Albi/reviews/)

### Inferences
- ASSUMED (standard industry practice): Lifecycle: FNOL call (often from insurer program, TPA or plumber referral) → dispatch within hours → inspection: category (1 clean, 2 gray, 3 black) and class (1–4 evaporation load) → signed work authorization plus assignment of benefits or direction to pay → mitigation (extraction, demo, drying chambers, equipment set) → daily monitoring visits (readings at the same points, equipment adjusted) → dry standard met → equipment pulled → certificate of completion signed → Xactimate estimate and invoice to the carrier (adjuster review, supplements) → rebuild or reconstruction job → collect deductible from the homeowner. Fire adds contents pack-out and inventory. Mold follows IICRC S520 (containment, HEPA, often third-party clearance testing). Roles: estimator/project manager, water techs, crew lead, office/claims coordinator, collections.
- ASSUMED: Nevada restoration work that goes into rebuild needs a Nevada State Contractors Board license. Mitigation-only licensing requirements are unverified.
- INFERRED: Restoration's biggest admin cost is getting paid: incomplete documentation causes carrier reductions and long receivables. A Kloomie restoration job needs per-day readings tied to fixed points on a floor plan, each piece of equipment's in/out dates (which bill per day), signed documents, and an auto-built drying report.

### Gaps
- DASH/Cotality and PSA features were not researched. Neither was SERVPRO's or ServiceMaster's internal workflow.
- Las Vegas restoration leaders (SERVPRO and Paul Davis franchises, local independents) were not identified with sources.
- IICRC S520 (mold) specifics and the 2021 S500 text itself were not read. They are paywalled.
- Xactimate pricing and Verisk integration terms were not verified.

## Junk removal and moving (incl. Nevada household goods rules)

### Takeaway
Junk removal is priced by truck volume (1-800-GOT-JUNK bills in eighth-truck increments with on-site final quotes). Intrastate household-goods movers in Nevada need NTA operating authority (CPCN) and a filed tariff, and their claims rules let a shipper appeal a claim denial to the NTA. College Hunks runs a Las Vegas franchise that does both junk and moving.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED (franchisor): 1-800-GOT-JUNK prices by the share of the truck used, "from a minimum of ⅛ of a truck all the way up to a full truckload", in eighth-truck increments, with single-item pricing for items like mattresses or fridges. Exact quotes are given on-site. Its 2026 guide lists half to full loads at about $600–$1,099; an older 2025 table shows minimum to quarter-truck at $107–$396 (ballpark, varies by market). — [1-800-GOT-JUNK pricing works](https://1800gotjunk.com/index%2Ephp/us_en/how-our-pricing-works); [1-800-GOT-JUNK pricing guide 2026](https://1800gotjunk.com/us_en/blog/pricing)
- VERIFIED: College Hunks Hauling Junk & Moving, Las Vegas: 5940 S. Rainbow Blvd., Suite 2014, Las Vegas NV 89118, Mon–Sat 8 AM–9 PM. Services: full-service moving, hourly moving, junk removal, donation pickup. Free no-obligation estimate before work. A 2021 press release said the franchisees serve Las Vegas, Henderson, Boulder City, The Lakes, North Las Vegas and Sloan. — [College Hunks Las Vegas](https://collegehunkshaulingjunk.com/las-vegas); [Franchising.com 2021](https://www.franchising.com/amp/news/20210802_college_hunks_hauling_junk__moving_experiences_vegas_market_boom.html)
- VERIFIED: Nevada intrastate household goods carriers file NTA tariffs tied to a CPCN. Household goods are defined by reference to NAC 706.067. The list itself was not retrieved. — [NRS 706.442 (Justia 2025)](https://law.justia.com/codes/nevada/chapter-706/statute-706-442/); [NAC 706 (rev. 4/13/2026)](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nAC/NAC-706.html)

### Inferences
- ASSUMED: Junk lifecycle: online booking of a two-hour arrival window → call-ahead → on-site volume estimate → customer approves → load → sort by donate, recycle or landfill → dump or transfer-station run (scale ticket and receipt kept per load to prove cost and disposal) → card payment on-site → donation receipt emailed. Bottlenecks: dump-fee reconciliation, quote disputes without photos, crew and truck utilization.
- ASSUMED: Moving lifecycle: inventory survey (virtual or in-home) → estimate under the filed tariff (hourly by crew size, plus travel time and materials) → booking with deposit → bill of lading signed before loading → inventory with condition codes for high-value items → delivery and signature → claims (photos, valuation coverage). ## Home inspections (Nevada licensing, report software)

### Takeaway
Nevada certifies home inspectors as "Inspectors of Structures" under NRS/NAC 645D through the Real Estate Division, in three tiers (residential, general, master), with classroom education, an exam, observed inspections, fingerprints and insurance. The work is booked ahead and agent-referred, and the report (Spectora, HomeGauge) is the product.

### Cited Findings

## Dispatch-driven vs booked-ahead, and what customers expect to receive

### Takeaway
Dispatch-driven (calls in, nearest truck): towing and roadside (motor clubs, police rotation), emergency water restoration, and same-day or rush courier. Booked ahead: recurring cleaning, janitorial contracts, grooming, detailing, inspections, moving and most junk removal (often same-day available). Customers in every trade expect photo proof plus an itemized receipt. Restoration and inspections add formal reports, and towing adds a regulated itemized bill.

## Software needs per trade (what a job record holds, stages, documents, top automations)

### Takeaway
Across all nine trades the shared core is customer + site + job + crew + photos + signature + itemized invoice + payment. The trade-specific pieces are listed below. Items are INFERRED from the findings above unless marked.

### Cited Findings
- (Every item below rests on the findings cited in the sections above. No new sources.)

### Inferences
- **Towing:** Record: call source (motor club with PO number, police via Autura, private property with signed request, cash), vehicle (VIN, plate, make/model/color, keys), pickup and drop locations, truck category A/B/C, day/night, timed services, storage class and start time, holds (police or evidence), lienholder. Stages: Call → Dispatched → En route → On scene → Hooked → Dropped/Impounded → Released / Lien process → Sold. Documents: itemized tariff bill, photos at hookup, private-property tow request, police notice, release form, lien notices. Automations: auto-pricing from the carrier's own NTA tariff table; daily storage accrual with the police-hold rate change after day 15; lien-clock timers (96 hours, 4 business days, 336 hours); motor club ETA and status updates; release checklist.
- **Cleaning (residential):** Record: home size, rooms list with per-room checklist, frequency, team, entry method + sealed key/alarm code, pets, preferences, price and extras. Stages: Quote → Booked → Reminder sent → In progress → Done (photos) → Charged → Review. Automations: recurring generation, reminders and on-my-way texts, auto-charge, re-clean ticket from a complaint with photos.
- **Janitorial (commercial):** Record: site, areas and per-area checklist, schedule per night, contract value, supply list. Stages: Walkthrough → Bid → Contract → Active (shifts) → Inspection → Monthly invoice. Documents: contract, scored inspection reports for the client. Automations: geofenced clock-in, missed-shift alert, scheduled inspections, monthly invoice.
- **Restoration:** Record: loss type, category and class, carrier, claim number, adjuster, deductible, floor plan with fixed reading points, daily readings, psychrometrics, equipment (serial and QR, in/out dates), affected materials, drying goal. Stages: FNOL → Dispatched → Inspected → Authorized → Mitigation → Monitoring (daily) → Dry → Equipment pulled → COC signed → Estimate submitted → Supplement → Paid. Documents: work authorization, AOB/direction to pay, daily drying logs, moisture map report, COC, Xactimate estimate. Automations: daily-reading reminders, auto drying report, equipment-day billing, flag readings not reaching goal by day 3, carrier A/R follow-ups.
- **Junk removal:** Record: volume fraction, items, before and after photos, donate/recycle/landfill split, disposal tickets (site, weight, fee). Stages: Booked → Arrived → Quoted on-site → Approved → Loaded → Dumped → Paid. Automations: window and arrival texts, receipt with donation note, dump-cost margin per job.
- **Moving (NV intrastate):** Record: inventory with condition codes and photos, crew size, hours and travel, materials, valuation, CPCN tariff rates. Stages: Survey → Estimate → Booked (deposit) → Bill of lading signed → Loaded → Delivered (signed) → Claim (if any). Automations: estimate priced from the filed tariff, claim intake with photos and due dates.
- **Courier:** Record: account (B2B), pickup and drop, window, package count and barcodes, POD (photo, signature, ID). Stages: Ordered → Routed → Picked up → In transit → Delivered / Exception. Automations: route batching by window, tracking links, POD emailed, monthly account statements.
- **Mobile grooming:** Record: pet profile (breed, weight, temperament, cut notes, photos), rabies vaccine with expiry, owner, address and parking/outlet notes. Stages: Booked → Vaccine checked → Reminder → Groomed (photos) → Paid → Rebooked. Automations: block booking on an expired vaccine, rebook prompt at checkout, route-by-day per van, before and after photo text.
- **Mobile detailing/repair:** Record: vehicle (VIN, size class), package and add-ons, water and power on site, walk-around photos signed, fleet account and vehicle list. Stages: Quote → Booked → On my way → Walk-around signed → Work → After photos → Paid. Automations: VIN decode, fleet recurring schedule with monthly invoice, tip prompt.
- **Home inspection:** Record: property, client and buyer's agent/listing agent, inspection type (buyer, pre-listing, add-ons), signed agreement, fee status, report with summary. Stages: Booked → Agreement signed → Paid → Inspected → Report published → Shared with agents → Repair request. Automations: hold the report until signed and paid, auto-send to the agent, agent referral tracking and thank-yous.
