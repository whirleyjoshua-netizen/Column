# Remodel and building trades: how they run in 2026 (painting, drywall/tile/finish carpentry, concrete/masonry, GC/remodel, insulation/waterproofing, handyman)

Research date: 2026-11-11. Las Vegas / Nevada first, then national. Each claim is labelled **VERIFIED** (source checked in this session, link inline) or **ASSUMED/INFERRED** (general industry knowledge or reasoning, not confirmed in this session). Most local-company and software sources are vendor or directory pages, so treat them as marketing claims. Statute text was read directly from leg.state.nv.us (current NRS 624 and NRS 108 pages).

## 1. Nevada licensing, contracts, liens and prompt pay (applies to every trade)

### Takeaway
In Nevada every paid trade job outside the small exemptions needs an NSCB license, and the license carries a dollar limit per job. Residential jobs need a written contract with set terms and a Residential Recovery Fund notice. Owners must pay within 21 days of a pay request when no schedule says otherwise, and retention is capped at 5%. Liens depend on a notice of right to lien, a 15-day notice of intent before recording on homes, recording within 90 days of completion, and the four statutory waiver forms. A Nevada trade app has to track license limits, contract terms, pay-request dates, lien notices and waivers per job.

### Cited Findings
**License structure and limits**
- **VERIFIED:** NRS 624.215 sets three branches: general engineering (A), general building (B) and specialty (C). General engineering and general building are "mutually exclusive." Under 624.215(3), general building generally means "more than two unrelated building trades or crafts." — [NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-624.html)
- **VERIFIED:** Under NRS 624.220(2), the Board must place a monetary limit on each license. The limit is the most a licensee may contract for on one site or subdivision for one client. Under 624.220(3), a request to raise it for one project is due at least 5 working days before bidding. — [NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-624.html)
- **VERIFIED:** Under NRS 624.220(4)(b), a licensee may do work outside its classification if the work is under $1,000 (labor and materials) and needs no permit. Under NRS 624.031(6), repair or maintenance under $1,000 is generally exempt from licensing unless it is part of a larger project of $500 or more. This is the "handyman exemption" that limits unlicensed handymen. — [NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-624.html)
- **VERIFIED (Board presentations, 2023 and 2024):** General B and B-2 licenses have a $200k minimum monetary limit. B-2 (residential and small commercial) is capped at 3 stories above ground and 1 below. Financial-statement tiers changed between the 2021 form ($10k/$50k/$250k) and the 2024 presentation ($25k/$500k/$1M). — [NSCB BAP presentation 2024](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Business-Assistance-Program-6.18.24.pdf); [NSCB 2023](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Business-Assistance-Program-Online-Presentation.pdf); [NV Legislature exhibit 2021](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/Session/81st2021/Exhibits/Senate/CL/SCL987G.pdf)
- **VERIFIED:** Bonds run from $1,000 to $500,000 depending on license type, limit and history. Recovery Fund assessments are based on the license's monetary limit. — [NSCB 2024 presentation](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Business-Assistance-Program-6.18.24.pdf)
- **VERIFIED:** There are two B-7 Residential Remodeling licenses:
  - The full B-7 (created by regulation in 2023) covers remodeling an existing single-family home or unit up to 3 stories. It does **not** cover additions that add enclosed space. — [NSCB B-7 bulletin](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/01-26-23-B-7-Residential-Remodeling-Classification.pdf); [NAC 624.170](https://law.cornell.edu/regulations/nevada/NAC-624-170)
  - SB 130 (effective Oct 1, 2025) created an entry-level **B-7 Restricted** license. Third-party sources cite about 2 years of experience, a bond of at least $2,000 and a reported $7,000 monetary limit. Confirm these figures with NSCB. — [NSCB SB 130 flyer](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/SB-130-Flyer.pdf); [NSCB 2026 restricted-license bulletin](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/03-17-2026-Restricted-License-Industry-Bulletin_2026.pdf); [RocketCert explainer](https://rocketcert.com/blog/168-construction/1833-nevada-b-7-residential-license-vs-b-7-restricted-license)
- **ASSUMED:** Specialty trades in this scope hold C licenses: painting C-4, concrete C-5, carpentry C-3, masonry C-18, tile C-20 (subclass), drywall/lathing C-11 or similar, insulation C-10/C-27 area, waterproofing under C-15/C-21 roofing-and-waterproofing families. Check exact codes against NAC 624.180–.190 before building pickers.

**Residential contract terms**
- **VERIFIED:** NRS 624.520 requires a residential contractor to tell the owner of their rights under the **Residential Recovery Fund**, with a written statement in substantially the statutory form in every contract for qualified services. The fund covers owner-occupied single-family homes and work by a Nevada licensee. — [NRS 624.520 (Justia)](https://law.justia.com/codes/nevada/2022/chapter-624/statute-624-520/); [NSCB consumer overview](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/?p=1283)
- **VERIFIED:** The fund pays up to **$40,000** per claim (NRS 624.510(3)). Total recoveries against one contractor are capped at the lesser of **$750,000 or 20%** of the account balance (624.510(7)). — [NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-624.html)
- **VERIFIED:** AB 39 (2023) added contract requirements for residential improvement work on single-family homes. They include the total contract amount with taxes and the Notice of Residential Recovery Fund. — [NSCB 2023 legislative bulletin](https://nvcontractorsboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/09-07-23-Legislative-Update-Industry-Bulletin.pdf)
- **VERIFIED (third-party guide, not confirmed in statute):** A residential contract missing the mandatory elements may be unenforceable against the owner. — [open-exam-prep NV GC guide](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/nv-general-contractor/contracts-residential-disclosures/mandatory-residential-disclosures)
- **VERIFIED:** Homeowners who use unlicensed contractors cannot claim from the Recovery Fund, and a contract with an unlicensed contractor is void. — [NSCB consumer overview](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/?p=1283)

**Residential pools and spas (NRS 624.900–.965; NAC 624.6958)**
- **VERIFIED:** For pool/spa contracts with a single-family owner over **$1,000**, NRS 624.940(2) requires:
  - the contractor's name, address and license number
  - an estimated completion date
  - a down payment of no more than **$1,000 or 10%** of the contract price, whichever is less
  - a progress-payment schedule that never exceeds 100% of the work's value
  - change orders that bind only if the owner signs them

  Other pool/spa rules:
  - With a down payment, work must start within 30 days after permits issue, unless the owner agrees in writing to a later start (624.935).
  - A finance-contingent contract has its own conditions (624.955).
  - Any term waiving these rights is void (624.940(5)).

  — [NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-624.html)
- **VERIFIED:** NAC 624.6958 requires pool/spa contracts and every change to be in writing, with some terms (such as the payment schedule) in at least 10-point bold. — [NAC 624.6958 (Justia)](https://regulations.justia.com/states/nevada/chapter-624/work-concerning-residential-pools-and-spas/section-624-6958); [NSCB "Taking the Plunge"](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/TakingThePlunge.pdf)
- **ASSUMED:** NRS 624.875–.900 covers residential photovoltaic contracts, not pools. The full NAC/NRS text on a general 3-business-day cancellation right for home improvement contracts was not confirmed this session. The federal FTC Cooling-Off Rule covers door-to-door sales of $25 or more at the home, and many in-home remodel sales trigger it.

**Prompt pay (NRS 624.606–.630)**
- **VERIFIED:** The owner pays on the written schedule. With no schedule, or an oral agreement, payment is due **21 days after the pay request** (624.609(1)). Retention is at most **5%**, and only if the contract allows it (624.609(2)). — [NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-624.html)
- **VERIFIED:** An owner who withholds must give signed written notice by the due date stating the amount, the reason and the basis. The prime may dispute it or fix the problem and give notice (624.609(3)–(4)). — [NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-624.html)
- **VERIFIED:** The prime may **stop work on 10 days' written notice** in three cases: late payment, no proper withholding notice, or no action on a change order within **30 days** (624.610(1)). After that the prime may terminate on 15 days' notice, or on 10 days' notice after a 15-day owner-caused stoppage. Lower tiers may stop with the prime. — [NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-624.html)
- **VERIFIED:** Higher tiers pay lower tiers by the earlier of two dates. With a written schedule, that is the scheduled date or **10 days after being paid**. With no schedule, it is **30 days after the request** or 10 days after being paid. Retention to subs is capped at 5% (624.624). A sub may stop work on 10 days' notice if unpaid 45 days after the 25th of the month the request went in (624.626). — [NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-624.html)

**Mechanic's liens (NRS 108)**
- **VERIFIED:** **Notice of Right to Lien** (NRS 108.245):
  - Every claimant except labor-only must give it to the owner, in person or by certified mail.
  - It may be given any time after first furnishing. The lien then reaches work done in the **31 days before** the notice and everything after.
  - A prime contractor, or anyone contracting directly with the owner, need not give it.
  - Subs and suppliers must copy the prime.
  - Without the notice, a lien for materials or other work cannot be perfected.

  — [NRS 108](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-108.html)
- **VERIFIED:** **NRS 108.246:** before signing a construction contract, each prime contractor must tell the owner about the 108.245 notice provisions in substantially the statutory form, and give a copy to each sub. Failing to do so is grounds for NSCB discipline. — [NRS 108](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-108.html)
- **VERIFIED:** **Recording the lien** (NRS 108.226): record within **90 days** after the latest of completion, last delivery or last work. If the owner records a valid **notice of completion**, the deadline is **40 days** after it records. — [NRS 108](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-108.html)
- **VERIFIED:** On single- or multi-family residences, a claimant other than a laborer must serve a **15-day notice of intent to lien** on the owner and prime before recording (personal delivery or certified mail). This extends the deadline by 15 days. Suppliers can't enforce without it (108.226(6)–(7)). — [NRS 108](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-108.html)
- **VERIFIED:** Serve a copy of the recorded lien on the owner within **30 days** (108.227). The owner mails the notice of completion within **10 days** of recording to each prime and each claimant who gave a preliminary notice, or it doesn't count against them (108.228). The lien expires **6 months** after recording unless suit is filed (108.233). — [NRS 108](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-108.html)
- **VERIFIED:** **Lien waivers** (NRS 108.2457): a waiver is unenforceable unless it is on one of the **four statutory forms**:
  - (a) conditional on progress payment
  - (b) unconditional on progress payment
  - (c) conditional on final payment
  - (d) unconditional on final payment

  Conditional forms take effect only when the check clears, and only for the amount paid. Unconditional forms must carry a conspicuous notice. A bounced check voids the waiver. Contract clauses that waive lien rights are void (108.2453). — [NRS 108](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-108.html)

### Inferences
- **INFERRED:** The usual practice for a small Nevada GC: collect a *conditional progress* waiver from each sub with its pay app, and an *unconditional progress* waiver once the check clears (for the prior period). On the last pay, collect *conditional final*, then *unconditional final*. The GC gives the owner its own waivers the same way. Software should generate all four NRS 108.2457 forms and track "conditional received → paid → unconditional received" per sub per draw.
- **INFERRED:** The monetary limit is a hard business rule. An estimate or contract above the license limit should warn, and a one-project raise must be requested 5 working days before the bid.
- **INFERRED:** Prompt-pay clocks (21 days owner → prime, 10 days prime → sub, 30-day change-order response) are natural automated reminders and "days outstanding" fields on each pay request.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed from statute: a general (non-pool) residential contract down-payment cap, a 3-day cancellation right, or the exact AB 39 element list. NRS 624 sections near 624.6956 and the NAC were too long to read fully this session. Use the NSCB residential contract checklist next.
- Exact C-subclass codes for tile, drywall, insulation and waterproofing were not checked against NAC 624.180+.
- The B-7 Restricted monetary limit ($7,000) comes from third parties only.

## 2. Permits, inspections, EPA RRP, Las Vegas conditions

### Takeaway
In the valley, the permit authority depends on where the address is: unincorporated Clark County, the City of Las Vegas, Henderson or North Las Vegas. Each has its own online portal: Clark County uses Accela Citizen Access, and North Las Vegas uses a Citizen Self-Service portal. Pre-1978 homes trigger EPA RRP paperwork. Summer heat drives pour and paint scheduling.

### Cited Findings
- **VERIFIED:** Unincorporated Clark County uses **Accela Citizen Access** (aca-prod.accela.com/CLARKCO). Users create an account, choose Building and apply. The county also offers "Building Simple Online Permits." — [Clark County Citizen Access guide](https://webfiles.clarkcountynv.gov/Building%20&%20Fire%20Prevention/IT/Knowledgebase/Citizen%20Access%20General%20Information.pdf); [Clark County Permit Issuance](https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/government/departments/building___fire_prevention/permit_issuance/index.php); [Submitting permits online](https://files.clarkcountynv.gov/Building%20&%20Fire%20Prevention/IT/Knowledgebase/Submitting%20for%20New%20Permits%20and%20Plans%20Online.pdf)
- **VERIFIED:** North Las Vegas uses a **Citizen Self-Service portal** that required users to re-register after a June 2025 update. — [North Las Vegas Permit Application Center](https://www.cityofnorthlasvegas.com/business/permit-application-center)
- **VERIFIED (third-party, low quality):** Henderson's "Online Permits Center / Citizen Access" handles permits and inspection scheduling. A "Las Vegas" mailing address doesn't settle the jurisdiction. — [buildingclub Henderson (beta)](https://zoning.buildingclub.info/nevada/henderson/building-permits); [permitplace Las Vegas](https://permitplace.com/city/las-vegas-nv-building-permits/)
- **ASSUMED:** None of these portals offers a public API for contractors. Contractors log in by hand, and permit numbers and inspection results are re-keyed into job software.
- **ASSUMED:** Typical residential remodel inspections run in this order: underground/footing (concrete), then underground plumbing, rough framing/electrical/plumbing/mechanical, insulation, drywall nail/screw (where required), shower pan/waterproofing, and final.
- **VERIFIED:** EPA RRP covers paid work that disturbs paint in pre-1978 homes, child-care facilities and preschools, including sole proprietors. — [EPA RRP contractors](https://www.epa.gov/node/255903); [EPA renovator compliance](https://epa.gov/lead/i-am-renovator-and-i-want-comply-epas-lead-renovation-repair-and-painting-rule-what-do-i-need)
  - The firm must be EPA-certified before advertising or doing covered work. Certification lasts **5 years** (40 CFR 745.89).
  - Each job needs a **certified renovator** assigned (745.90).
  - The **Renovate Right** pamphlet goes to the occupant before work starts.
  - Lead paint is presumed unless testing shows it is absent.
- **ASSUMED (from rule text known before this session):**
  - Typical "minor repair" de minimis thresholds: 6 sq ft interior per room, 20 sq ft exterior.
  - RRP records (pamphlet receipt, renovator's checklist, cleaning verification) must be kept **3 years**.
  - Nevada is not one of the 14 states that run their own program, so EPA administers RRP there. Confirm this.
- **VERIFIED (vendor/contractor blogs, not standards):**
  - ACI 305 hot-weather guidance is often read as a 90–95°F limit on concrete *temperature at placement*.
  - Las Vegas is above 90°F air temperature from late May to mid-October, with 110–115°F highs in June–August.
  - Crews pour at 5–6 a.m. or at night, use chilled water or ice, and build 1–2 weather-hold days per week into summer schedules.

  — [concretepourchecker ACI 305/306](https://concretepourchecker.com/guides/concrete-temperature-limits-aci-305r-306r); [Centurion Concrete LV](https://centurionconcretelasvegas.com/concrete-guides/best-time-pour-concrete-las-vegas/); [1 Home Construction LV climate](https://1homeconstruction.com/las-vegas-construction-climate/)
- **VERIFIED (blogs, conflicting):** Caliche bears loads well but is hard to dig. It raises excavation cost and footing time. One source claims valley caliche/clay can heave slabs 1–2 inches, which conflicts with the "excellent bearing" description. — [ConcreteToolkit Nevada](https://concretetoolkit.com/state/nevada-concrete-calculator/); [1 Home Construction](https://1homeconstruction.com/las-vegas-construction-climate/)
- **ASSUMED:** Exterior painting in summer is limited by surface temperature. Most manufacturer data sheets cap application around 90–95°F surface or air, so crews paint shaded elevations and follow the sun, starting at dawn. Monsoon (July–Sept) dust storms and rain cause holds.

### Inferences
- **INFERRED:** Weather is a first-class scheduling input in Las Vegas. Jobs need pour windows (early start), "hold if forecast > X°F," and auto-rescheduling of dependent steps (forms → pour → strip → cure days → next trade).
- **INFERRED:** Every job record should have a "year built" field that decides whether RRP applies. Pre-1978 adds a checklist (pamphlet signed, certified renovator named, containment photos, cleaning verification) and keeps records for 3 years.

### Gaps
- No primary ACI 305 text or paint manufacturer data sheets were read. City of Las Vegas and Henderson portal names were not confirmed from official pages.

## 3. How each trade runs: workflow, roles, lifecycle, terms

### Takeaway
All six trades follow the same skeleton: lead → site visit/measure → estimate → contract + deposit → (permit) → schedule → work in phases with inspections → change orders → progress or final billing → punch list → final payment → warranty. They differ in how big a job is, how many phases it has, and whether there are subs and draws. A GC remodel is many phases with subs and draws. Painting and concrete are one or two phases with a deposit and final payment. Handyman work is many small tasks in a single visit.

### Cited Findings and assumed operating models
**General contracting / remodels (B-2, B-7)**
- **VERIFIED (local firms' public claims):** Leading LV/Henderson remodelers market **design-build** kitchen and bath work, with awards (Best of Houzz, NARI Remodeler of the Year, RJ Best of Las Vegas) and B-2 licenses:
  - Las Vegas Remodel & Construction (B-2 #51092, Best of Houzz since 2016, A+ BBB) — [Lantern](https://lantern.llc/b/las-vegas-remodel-construction-henderson/book)
  - Kingdom & Co. (design-build; NARI Remodeler of the Year 2025/2026 per Houzz) — [Houzz](https://houzz.com/pro/kingdomandco)
  - Tridel Construction (Qualified Remodeler Top 500 since 2020, full B) — [Houzz](https://www.houzz.com/pro/tridelco/)
  - Acquired Designs (8× Best of Las Vegas) — [Lantern](https://lantern.llc/b/acquired-designs-home-remodeling-henderson/book)
  - Home Design Construction (RJ Best of LV 2025 Bronze, 4.9/183 Google) — [Lantern](https://lantern.llc/b/home-design-construction-las-vegas/book)
- **ASSUMED:** **Roles:**
  - The owner/GC sells and estimates in small firms.
  - Larger firms add a designer, an estimator, a project manager, and a superintendent or lead carpenter on site.
  - An office manager handles scheduling and selections.
  - A bookkeeper handles AP/AR, job costing and lien waivers.
  - Subs cover plumbing, electrical, HVAC, drywall, tile, paint, countertops and glass.
- **ASSUMED:** **Lifecycle:**
  1. Lead, then a phone qualifier on budget and timeline.
  2. Site visit and measure.
  3. Design agreement or retainer (design-build).
  4. Selections and allowances.
  5. Fixed-price or cost-plus estimate, then contract with the Recovery Fund notice, the lien-notice disclosure (NRS 108.246) and the payment schedule.
  6. Deposit.
  7. Permit (Accela or city portal).
  8. Pre-construction meeting, then order long-lead items (cabinets 6–12 weeks).
  9. Demo, rough-in, inspections, insulation, drywall, finishes, final inspection.
  10. Punch/walkthrough, final payment, 1-year workmanship warranty.
- **ASSUMED:** **Terms:** allowance, selections, change order (CO), cost-plus vs fixed bid, markup vs margin, draw schedule, schedule of values (SOV), retainage, pay app (AIA G702/G703 on larger jobs), RFI, submittal, punch list, substantial completion, CO (certificate of occupancy, only for additions or new builds), COI, W-9, 1099-NEC.

**Painting (C-4)**
- **VERIFIED:** National franchises are active in Las Vegas. CertaPro Painters of Summerlin/West LV claims top LV painting company for 2024 and 2025 and Best of Las Vegas 2025. CertaPro Southern Nevada markets commercial and senior-care painting. Local independents include Nevada Painting Company (merged with Vegas Painters), Allpro Painters (22 years) and Straight Edge Painting. — [CertaPro Summerlin](https://certapro.com/summerlin/?p=10662); [CertaPro Southern Nevada](https://certapro.com/southern-nevada/?p=1449); [Lantern LV painters](https://lantern.llc/nv/las-vegas/home-services/painting); [Lantern CertaPro](https://lantern.llc/b/certapro-painters-of-summerlin-west-las-vegas/book)
- **VERIFIED:** PaintScout prices work with **production rates** (time-based rates per surface: walls, trim, cabinets) from dimensions and counts. It includes presentation-style proposals, CRM, scheduling, production management, deposits and final payments (ACH/card) and Wisetack financing. Setup costs $999 (Plus) or $1,499 (Pro). The vendor claims a 23% average win-rate lift. — [PaintScout](https://www.paintscout.com); [PaintScout painting estimates](https://www.paintscout.com/painting-estimates); [PaintScout presentations](https://www.paintscout.com/presentations)
- **ASSUMED:**
  - **Workflow:** an in-home estimate measured per room or per elevation, an options-based proposal (good/better/best), and a deposit of about 10–30%.
  - **Before the start:** color selection and job-start check-in (HOA approval of exterior colors is common in Las Vegas master-planned communities).
  - **Crew:** a crew lead with a production sheet (hours budget), surface prep, then coats.
  - **Close-out:** a daily photo and progress text, a walkthrough with blue-tape punch, final payment and a review request.
  - **Pre-1978:** RRP.
- **ASSUMED:** **Roles:** owner, estimator/sales, production manager, crew leads, painters (often 1099 subs in small shops).

**Drywall / tile / finish carpentry (C-3/C-11/C-20 families)**
- **ASSUMED:**
  - Usually subs to GCs, paid per piece or per unit: drywall per board or sq ft hung/taped, tile per sq ft by size and pattern, trim per linear ft or per door/window.
  - **Workflow:** takeoff from plans or a site measure, a bid to the GC, scheduling into the GC's sequence (after insulation inspection, before paint), material delivery, then work in stages: hang → tape → texture (Las Vegas homes are commonly orange-peel or knockdown).
  - **Tile:** waterproofing or shower pan inspection before tile.
  - **Billing:** progress invoices to the GC with lien waivers and back-charges for damage.
  - **Terms:** level 0–5 finish, texture match, LF, SF, waste factor, thinset, membrane (Schluter/RedGard), flood test.

**Concrete and masonry (C-5, C-18)**
- **ASSUMED:**
  - **Work:** driveways, patios, footings, block walls (CMU walls are ubiquitous in Las Vegas yards), pavers and stucco-adjacent work.
  - **Workflow:** site visit; check for caliche (may need a jackhammer or rock trencher, priced as a contingency); quote per sq ft or per LF of wall.
  - **Before the pour:** permit for walls over the height threshold and structural footings; Utility locate (811); forms and rebar, then a footing inspection before the pour.
  - **Pour:** order a ready-mix truck for an early-morning slot; finish; saw-cut joints within hours; cure.
  - **Close-out:** strip forms, seal, final.
  - **Payment:** a deposit for materials and the balance on completion.
- **VERIFIED (cost guide, unsourced):** Las Vegas patios cost about $8–$14/sq ft in 2026. — [slabcalc LV patio](https://www.slabcalc.co/cost/patio/las-vegas-nv)
- **ASSUMED:** **Terms:** PSI mix, slump, yards, rebar/mesh, control joint, broom finish, stamped, cure time, cold joint, pump truck, CMU, bond beam, grout, cap.

**Insulation and waterproofing**
- **ASSUMED:**
  - **Insulation:** priced per sq ft at an R-value (attic blown-in, batts, spray foam), with utility rebate paperwork (NV Energy programs) and an insulation inspection on remodels and new work.
  - **Waterproofing:** deck coatings, roof and foundation membranes, shower pans. Warranty registration with the membrane manufacturer is a key document.
  - **Both trades:** often one-day jobs with before/after photos and moisture or depth readings as proof.

**Handyman**
- **VERIFIED:** NRS 624.031(6) exempts repair or maintenance under $1,000 unless it is part of a larger project of $500 or more. Work needing a permit needs a license (624.220(4)(b)). — [NRS 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-624.html)
- **VERIFIED (cost guides, mixed quality):**
  - Flat rates suit clearly scoped tasks and are padded above the hourly rate. Hourly suits honey-do lists of many small tasks.
  - Some shops sell fixed blocks (for example, 4-hour visits).
  - Typical US rates are $75–$125/hr, with minimums around 1.5–2.5 hours.
  - Franchises charge more because of overhead and insurance.

  — [Jobber pricing guide](https://getjobber.com/academy/handyman/how-to-price-handyman-jobs/); [Angi price list](https://www.angi.com/articles/what-are-common-handyman-prices.htm); [Thumbtack](https://www.thumbtack.com/k/household/); [honey-do demo page](https://fulcrum-handyman-demo-site.netlify.app/services/honey-do-list/)
- **ASSUMED:** **Franchises:** Mr. Handyman (Neighborly), Ace Handyman Services (Ace Hardware) and Handyman Connection sell scheduled visits by time block (often half day or full day) with a task list captured at booking, with photos uploaded beforehand. The tech gets the list, parts are pre-staged, and the customer is billed per hour or block plus materials with markup at the end of the visit. Larger tasks are converted into separate quotes. Franchise pricing is not public; no franchise page was reviewed this session.

### Inferences
- **INFERRED:** A shared "job" model works if phases are configurable: one phase for concrete/painting, ten-plus for a GC remodel, and a task list for handyman work.
- **INFERRED:** The Las Vegas-specific differentiators are HOA approval steps (colors, block walls), caliche contingency line items, heat-window scheduling, and the four Nevada waiver forms.

### Gaps

## 4. Change orders, draws, lien waivers, subs, handyman scheduling, software

### Takeaway
In practice, a small GC runs money as deposit → milestone draws against a schedule of values → signed change orders priced with markup → retainage held, if any → final payment after the punch list. Each sub pay is tied to the sub's waiver and a current COI. Software leaders (Buildertrend, JobTread, Procore for larger GCs, PaintScout for painters, Jobber/Housecall Pro for handymen) compete on client portals, selections, change orders, daily logs and payments.

Handymen use Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan (larger) or Markate.

**How change orders work in practice (ASSUMED, standard practice)**
1. Something discovered on site (rot, caliche) or a client request.
2. The lead photographs and notes it.
3. The PM prices labor and material plus markup (commonly 15–25%), and adds schedule days.
4. The CO is sent for e-signature *before* work.
5. On signing, the contract total, the SOV/draws and the sub's PO are updated.
6. It is billed on the next draw or due at signing.

In Nevada pool/spa contracts, a CO is unenforceable unless the owner signs it (VERIFIED for pools, NRS 624.940). Unapproved COs over 30 days give the prime a stop-work right (VERIFIED, NRS 624.610).

**Draws (ASSUMED)**
- Residential remodels use milestone draws (for example: 10% deposit → 25% at rough-in passed → 25% at drywall → 25% at cabinets/tile → 15% at final).
- Bank-financed jobs use a lender's draw inspection and title company disbursement with lien waivers.
- Nevada caps retainage at 5% and requires payment in 21 days with no schedule (VERIFIED above).

**Sub onboarding and scheduling (ASSUMED)**
- **Documents collected:**
  - W-9 (for the 1099-NEC)
  - Certificate of insurance: general liability with the GC as additional insured, workers' comp or a sole-proprietor exemption, auto
  - Nevada license number and monetary limit (checked on the NSCB lookup)
  - Business license (Clark County or city)
  - Signed subcontract or master agreement
  - Lien waiver per payment
  - RRP renovator certificate if the job is pre-1978
- **Expiry tracking:** COIs expire yearly, and an expired COI blocks payment and scheduling.
- **Scheduling:** subs are booked by text or phone against a Gantt with finish-to-start dependencies (rough plumbing → inspection → insulation → inspection → drywall). They confirm or decline, and a slip pushes downstream tasks.

**Handyman many-task visits (ASSUMED)**
- At booking, the office captures each task with photos and estimates its minutes from a task library.
- It totals the tasks to a visit length (2h, 4h, 8h blocks) and checks the tech's skills and truck stock.
- Parts are pre-bought; the tech works the checklist in priority order.
- Unfinished items roll to a return visit. Out-of-scope or over-$1,000 or permit work becomes a separate quote, or goes to a licensed partner.
- At the end of the visit, the tech invoices on the spot with photos and card payment.

## Software needs per trade (summary; all INFERRED from the above)

**GC / remodel**
- **Job record:** client, address, jurisdiction (County / LV / Henderson / NLV), permit numbers, year built (RRP), HOA, contract type (fixed/cost-plus), license monetary limit check, budget/SOV, allowances, selections, subs per phase.
- **Stages:** lead → visit → design → estimate → contract → deposit → permit → pre-con → demo → rough-in → rough inspections → insulation → drywall → finishes → final inspection → punch → final pay → warranty.
- **Documents:** contract with Recovery Fund notice and NRS 108.246 lien disclosure, COs, permits, inspection results, sub COI/W-9/license, 4 NRS 108.2457 waiver forms, daily logs, photos, warranty letter.
- **Money:** deposit → milestone draws (5% retainage cap) → COs added on signature → sub pays (10-day rule) with waivers → final.
- **Top automations:** CO e-sign updates the totals; draw invoice and waiver packet on milestone; COI expiry alerts that block payment; dependency reschedule; prompt-pay reminders; a weekly homeowner update from daily logs and photos.

**Painting**
- **Job record:** rooms or elevations with measurements, production-rate estimate, colors/products per area, HOA approval, year built.
- **Stages:** lead → estimate visit → options proposal → deposit → color confirm → schedule → prep → coats → walkthrough → final pay → review request.
- **Documents:** proposal, color sheet, RRP pamphlet and checklist (pre-1978), photos.
- **Money:** deposit plus balance on completion.
- **Top automations:** production-rate estimating, auto job-start and day-before texts, a heat or weather hold, and an RRP checklist triggered by year built.

**Drywall / tile / finish carpentry (sub to GC)**
- **Job record:** GC, takeoff quantities (SF/LF/boards), unit prices, finish level or texture, material deliveries.
- **Stages:** bid → award → scheduled after insulation inspection → hang/set → finish → pan or flood test (tile) → punch.
- **Documents:** subcontract, own COI and W-9 to the GC, waivers per pay.
- **Money:** progress invoices to the GC, back-charges, retainage.
- **Top automations:** takeoff to unit-price bid, GC schedule sync, pay-app plus waiver generation.

**Concrete / masonry**
- **Job record:** sq ft or LF, PSI/mix, yards, rebar, finish, caliche contingency, 811 ticket, permit (walls/footings), pour date and time window.
- **Stages:** visit → quote → deposit → permit/locate → forms/rebar → footing inspection → pour → cut/cure → strip/seal → final.
- **Documents:** quote, permit, inspection, ready-mix ticket, photos.
- **Money:** deposit for materials plus balance at completion (draws for big walls).
- **Top automations:** heat-window scheduling (early pours, hold if over the limit), cure-day dependency, inspection-to-pour gating, supplier order reminders.

**Insulation / waterproofing**
- **Job record:** area, R-value or product, measurements, rebate eligibility, manufacturer warranty IDs.
- **Stages:** quote → schedule → install → inspection (if required) → rebate forms → warranty registration.
- **Documents:** product certificates, rebate paperwork, warranty.
- **Money:** usually paid in full at completion (rebate assignment possible).
- **Top automations:** rebate and warranty forms prefilled from the job, before/after photo proof.

**Handyman**
- **Job record:** a task list (each with photo, estimated minutes and materials), visit block length, tech skills, and a $1,000 / permit flag that pushes the work to a licensed quote.
- **Stages:** request → task capture → visit sizing → booking → parts → visit → invoice on site → follow-ups for leftover tasks.
- **Documents:** task photos before/after, invoice.
- **Money:** hourly or block rate plus materials with markup, card on site, optional membership or maintenance plans.
- **Top automations:** task library to visit length, auto-scheduling of route-efficient visits, on-the-spot invoice and payment, and splitting large tasks into quotes.
