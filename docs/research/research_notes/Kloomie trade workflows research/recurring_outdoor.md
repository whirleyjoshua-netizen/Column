# Recurring outdoor and home services (lawn/landscape, tree, pest, pool/spa, irrigation, snow, window/pressure/gutter): how they run in 2026, Las Vegas first

Research date: 2026-11-11. Every bullet under "Cited Findings" carries a source. Most sources are vendor pages, directories or local news, and each bullet says which. Bullets under "Inferences" are ASSUMED/INFERRED. They come from how these trades usually run and were not checked against a source in this pass. The research stopped at about 15 searches, so many workflow details are inferred and listed as such.

## 1. Las Vegas / Henderson rules that shape the work (SNWA watering, AB 356 turf, SNHD pools, NDA pest licensing)

### Takeaway
Four local rules affect the schedules and records that software must hold:
- **SNWA watering groups.** Watering days are assigned by season and by group, and Sundays are never allowed.
- **AB 356.** It bans Colorado River water on nonfunctional turf at non-single-family properties from 2027-01-01, so HOA and commercial turf removal is a 2026 rush.
- **SNHD pools.** Anyone servicing public or commercial pool chemistry must register with SNHD, meet a 2-hour response time and keep maintenance logs.
- **NDA pest licenses.** Licenses come in field and category tiers, including "Limited Landscape" for landscapers who apply pesticides.

### Cited Findings
**SNWA watering schedule (VERIFIED, news and SNWA)**
- Spring (Mar 1 to Apr 30): 3 assigned days a week. Summer (May 1 to Aug 31): more days allowed, but no watering from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. Fall (Sep 1 to Oct 31): 3 assigned days a week, and drip-watered trees and plants should stay at 2 days. Winter (Nov 1 to Feb 28): 1 assigned day a week. Sundays are always off. — [Fox5, summer 2026](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/04/29/southern-nevadas-summer-watering-schedule-begins-may-1/?outputType=amp); [Fox5, fall 2026](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/09/07/fall-watering-restrictions-now-effect-southern-nevada/); [SNWA](https://snwa.com/); [Lawn by Season (secondary)](https://lawnbyseason.com/water-restrictions/nevada/las-vegas-nv)
- Days are set by the watering group assigned to each address, which can be looked up on snwa.com. Utilities (LVVWD, Henderson) have their own lookups. Watering outside your days brings water-waste fees. — [SNWA watering group](https://www.snwa.com/conservation/watering-group/index.html?lang=en) (SSL certificate expired when fetched; content taken from search snippet); [SNWA change your clock](https://snwa.com/conservation/change-your-irrigation-clock/index.html); [LVVWD service rules ch.10](https://www.lvvwd.com/assets/pdf/service-rules-chapter10.pdf)
- One source conflict: a 2025 Las Vegas Sun story put the winter start on a Saturday, so the exact changeover day differs by year and source. — [Las Vegas Sun](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2025/oct/30/southern-nevada-water-authority-implements-winter/)

**AB 356 nonfunctional turf (VERIFIED)**
- Nevada AB 356 (2021) bans Colorado River water for watering nonfunctional turf on all properties except single-family homes from **January 1, 2027**. In practice, removal must be done by 2026-12-31. It covers HOA common areas and commercial sites. SNWA also counts strips in ingress/egress easements as nonfunctional. — [NV Real Estate Division AB356 guide](https://red.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/rednvgov/Content/CIC/Publications/AB356.pdf); [City of Henderson AB356](https://www.cityofhenderson.com/government/departments/utility-services/water-conservation/useless-grass-removal-ab356); [Barchart/press release](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/734350/southern-nevada-businesses-and-hoas-face-2027-nonfunctional-turf-deadline)
- Rebate: the $3/sq ft rate applied only to projects finished by 2024-12-31. Since 2025-01-01 it is $2/sq ft for the first 10,000 sq ft and $1/sq ft after that. A conflicting source: FS Residential still says "up to $3." — [red.nv.gov](https://red.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/rednvgov/Content/CIC/Publications/AB356.pdf); contradicted by [FS Residential](https://www.fsresidential.com/nevada/news-events/articles/navigating-nevada-turf-restrictions-ab-356/)
- In Sep 2026, LVVWD proposed fines that could pass $5,100 per violation from 2027-01-01. This is a proposal, not a final rule. A class action is trying to halt enforcement. — [Fox5 2026-09-30](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/09/30/las-vegas-valley-water-district-proposes-fines-strict-rules-non-functional-turf/); [News3LV](https://news3lv.com/news/local/story/court-rulings-loom-in-las-vegas-dispute-over-water-authority-turf-removal-rules)

**SNHD aquatic health (VERIFIED, partly from older documents)**
- SNHD's Aquatic Facility Regulations (2018 version found) set minimum operating standards for public pools and spas in Clark County, including chemical storage and use. — [SNHD 2018 Aquatic Regulations](https://www.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/download/aquatic-health/Regualtions/20180705-Aquatic-Regulations.pdf)
- SNHD 2022 industry outreach: anyone who maintains public aquatic venue chemistry needs **SNHD registration, a 2-hour response time, and maintenance logs**. — [SNHD 2022 outreach slides](https://media.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/download/aquatics/20221215-meeting/20221215-Aquatic-Industry-Outreach-Meeting.pdf)
- Test frequency (2019 training): total alkalinity weekly. Cyanuric acid monthly if added, weekly if stabilized chlorine is used. Free chlorine 1.0 to 10.0 ppm when no CYA is used. — [SNHD 2019 training deck](https://www.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/download/aquatic-health/2019/2019-Jan-AHP-Pool-Regulation-Training.pdf)
- SNHD publishes a daily or monthly Pool/Spa report form that asks for the operator or service company, a phone number and the disinfectant used. The daily log covers disinfectant residual, pH and maintenance done (Review-Journal, older). — [SNHD pool-spa daily report](https://www.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/download/eh/swimmingpool/pool-spa-daily-report.pdf); [Review-Journal HOA pool tips (older)](https://www.reviewjournal.com/?p=1365645)
- These rules apply to **public** (HOA, apartment, hotel) pools. A private backyard pool has no SNHD log requirement (INFERRED from the scope of the regulations).

**Nevada Department of Agriculture pest licensing (VERIFIED from NDA forms; recordkeeping details partly unconfirmed)**
- Licensing has three fields: Aerial, Agricultural Ground and Urban/Structural. The business license packet lists categories under A (Aerial), B (Agricultural Ground) and C (Urban/Structural). The old Ornamental & Turf category, minus weed control, is now **Limited Landscape**. — [NDA Pest Control Licensing Guide](https://agri.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/agrinvgov/Content/Resources/Forms/Plant/Pest/Pest%20Control%20Licensing%20Guide.pdf); [NDA Business License Packet](https://agri.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/agrinvgov/Content/Resources/Forms/Plant/Pest/Business%20License%20Application%20Packet.pdf); [NDA 2015 pesticide regs fact sheet](https://agri.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/agrinvgov/Content/Media/2015-02-19-Pesticide%20Regs.pdf)
- License tiers: a business license (needs a company name application and a certificate of insurance), a **Principal** license (needs 2 years of application experience, or 6 months plus 16 college credits, and an exam in the categories of that experience), and ground applicator licenses. NDA does not waive testing. — [NDA Principal Test Application](https://agri.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/agrinvgov/Content/Resources/Forms/Plant/Pest/Principal%20Test%20Application.pdf); [NDA Ground License Application](https://agri.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/agrinvgov/Content/Plant/PEST/Forms/Ground%20License%20Application.pdf); [NDA exam contents](https://agri.nv.gov/Plant/PEST/Exam_Contents_Retesting)
- NAC 555 covers recordkeeping. A 2021 revision (R077-21) concerns records of general-use pesticide applications. — [NDA R077-21 draft](https://agri.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/agri.nv.gov/Content/Plant/Workshop_and_Hearing_Meetings/R077-21RP1%20(002).pdf); [NAC 555 overview](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/nv-pesticide-applicator/nevada-pesticide-laws/nv-statutes-admin-code)
- Renewal rules conflict between third-party sources ("every 2 years" versus "12 CEUs per 4 years"). Not confirmed against NDA. — [Housecall Pro NV licensing (third-party)](https://www.housecallpro.com/licensing/pest-control/nevada/)

### Inferences
- **Irrigation and landscape software in Las Vegas should store each property's SNWA watering group and season**, and set reminders to change controller clocks at the season changes (Mar 1, May 1, Sep 1, Nov 1). A "clock change" round of visits four times a year is an easy recurring upsell. (INFERRED)
- AB 356 makes 2026 to early 2027 a peak year for **turf-conversion projects** at HOAs and commercial sites. These are one-off projects that turn into recurring desert-landscape maintenance contracts, so software should support "project → recurring agreement." Rebate paperwork (before and after photos, square footage) is part of the job. (INFERRED)
- Landscapers who spray weeds or fertilize need an NDA license (Limited Landscape or more), so a landscape visit record that includes spraying needs pesticide-application fields just like pest control. (INFERRED from the category structure)

### Gaps
- Exact NAC 555 record fields: the R077-21 final text wasn't retrieved. The record probably includes date, location, applicator and licence number, product and EPA registration number, amount, target pest and site (ASSUMED from the usual pattern in other states). Confirm in NAC 555.
- The current SNHD regulation version (post-2018), and whether 2024 Board of Health aquatic changes altered the log requirements ([SNHD BOH May 2024 item](https://media.southernnevadahealthdistrict.org/download/boh24/20240523-BOH/Item-X-aquatic.pdf), not read).
- Nevada State Contractors Board licensing for pool repair/build and landscape contractors (C-10, C-5 etc.) was not researched.
- The exact summer days per week (often stated as no limit on days but time-of-day limits) wasn't confirmed from SNWA directly.

## 3. Pool and spa: what the tech logs each visit, what the customer gets, routes and billing

### Takeaway
Pool is the most data-heavy recurring visit. Each stop records water readings for each body of water (pool and spa are separate), the chemicals and amounts added, a checklist and photos. The customer then gets an automatic email, a "digital door hanger," with the recap. Leading tools auto-calculate doses from target readings and run drag-and-drop daily routes on a weekly cadence.

### Cited Findings
- Skimmer manages customers, service stops, routes, techs, service reports, invoices, parts purchasing and chemical readings. It has built-in chemical logs and service history **for each body of water**. — [GetApp AU: Skimmer](https://www.getapp.com.au/software/113919/skimmer); [Skimmer blog](https://www.getskimmer.com/blog/how-pool-service-software-helps-you-get-organized-and-save-time)
- Skimmer's "digital door hangers" are automatic emails that recap each visit for the customer. Service reports can carry the logo, chemical readings and doses, pool photos and finished checklist items. — [Skimmer blog](https://getskimmer.com/blog/best-pool-maintenance-software); [Capterra AU compare](https://www.capterra.com.au/compare/177014/194225/skimmer/vs/pool-brain)
- Dosing: Skimmer tells the tech exactly what chemicals to add and how much, based on the company's dosing preferences. Pool Brain fills in dose amounts automatically from saved target readings. — [Software Advice AU compare](https://www.softwareadvice.com.au/compare/89281/313522/skimmer/vs/pool-brain); [Pool Brain vs Skimmer (vendor)](https://poolcompanysoftware.poolbrain.com/skimmer-vs-poolbrain)
- Routes: Skimmer shows a map with travel times calculated automatically and drag-and-drop reordering. Pool Brain shows live route progress bars and supports multi-day scheduling. — same sources as above
- Pricing (vendor listings): Skimmer from $49/mo, Pool Brain from $10/mo. Reviews: Skimmer 4.9 from about 226 to 228 reviews, Pool Brain 4.9 from 27 (Mar 2026). — [GetApp: Skimmer](https://www.getapp.com/industries-software/a/skimmer/); [Capterra Skimmer reviews](https://capterra.com/p/177014/Skimmer/reviews/)
- For public pools (SNHD), the log needs disinfectant residual and pH (daily), total alkalinity (weekly) and CYA (monthly or weekly), plus registration and a 2-hour response time. — see Section 1 sources

### Inferences
- The per-visit log fields are the standard set in pool software (ASSUMED and not field-checked from vendor docs): free chlorine and total chlorine (combined chlorine is derived), pH, total alkalinity, calcium hardness, CYA, salt (for salt systems), phosphates, water temperature and TDS. Then chemicals added (product, amount, unit, cost), checklist items (skim, brush, vacuum, empty baskets, backflush/clean filter, check equipment), filter pressure, before and after photos, notes and gate code.
- **Las Vegas hard water:** very high calcium hardness and TDS mean regular calcium scale on tile lines and salt cells, periodic acid washes or drain-and-refills, and tile-cleaning upsells (ASSUMED; the Las Vegas hard-water point is backed only indirectly by the Thumbtack window note). Logging calcium hardness over time lets software suggest a drain-and-refill.
- **Monsoon (Jul to Sep):** dust storms and debris cause extra stops, filter cleans and algae treatments. Software needs "add an extra visit to all route X stops" and a storm-surcharge line (INFERRED).
- Billing: most residential pool service is a **monthly flat rate** for weekly service, with chemicals either included or billed per use. Repairs (pumps, filters, heaters, salt cells) are separate quoted jobs. Charging is by autopay or batch invoicing at month end (ASSUMED industry norm; Skimmer lists invoicing).
- Skipped visits: a "skipped" or "can't access" status with a reason (locked gate, dog, customer request), a photo of the locked gate, and an automatic notice to the customer (INFERRED from the door-hanger feature).

### Gaps
- Exact Skimmer and Pool Brain visit-record fields and LSI (Langelier saturation index) support were not checked on vendor feature pages.
- PHTA/CPO (Certified Pool Operator) credential requirements in Nevada were not researched.
- No verified data on typical Las Vegas weekly pool service prices.

## 4. Pest control: workflow, compliance records, software

### Takeaway
Pest is recurring by agreement: monthly, bi-monthly or quarterly, often with an initial "clean-out" visit at a higher price and free re-services between visits. The compliance core is the pesticide-application record (product, EPA registration number, concentration, amount, site, applicator licence), so software must track materials. PestPac leads on state-report depth, FieldRoutes on route optimization and GorillaDesk on small operators.

### Cited Findings
- PestPac stores every chemical with its EPA registration number, concentration and undiluted quantity. Third parties say it has 30+ state-specific exports (California, Arizona TARF etc.). Its price is quote-only, about $150 per user per month (third-party). — [PestPac material tracking](https://www.pestpac.com/features/material-tracking-software); [G2 discussion](https://www.g2.com/discussions/how-does-pestpac-handle-chemical-tracking-and-compliance-documentation-for-state-audits)
- FieldRoutes: techs log chemical use in the mobile app, and the office pulls inventory and state chemical reports from it. A third party says FieldRoutes is stronger on routing and PestPac stronger on state DOA reports. — [FieldRoutes blog](https://fieldroutes.com/blog/pest-control-pesticide-usage-tracking-apps); [pipeline-marketing comparison (affiliate-style)](https://pipeline-marketing.pages.dev/blog/pest-control-software/)
- GorillaDesk generates state-required pesticide usage logs "in a few clicks." A third party says this is on the Pro tier and up, priced at $49 to $149 per route per month. — [GorillaDesk chemical tracking](https://gorilladesk.com/features/chemical-tracking-software/); [tooleduppro review](https://tooleduppro.pages.dev/reviews/gorilladesk)
- Las Vegas frequency: monthly while a problem is active, then every other month. Scorpion homes usually choose monthly. — [Burns](https://www.burnspestelimination.com/service-areas/pest-control-las-vegas-nv); [Pest Control Inc](https://pestcontrolinc-site.pages.dev/pest-control-las-vegas/plans-and-pricing/)

### Inferences
- Visit record (ASSUMED from the pest-software norm):
  - target pests and areas treated (interior/exterior, perimeter, eaves, yard)
  - each product (name, EPA registration number, dilution, amount, method)
  - weather and wind for exterior work, applicator name and NDA licence number
  - device or bait-station checks (for rodent and termite stations, by station number)
  - pest activity seen, conditions found (conducive conditions)
  - photos, customer signature, and a re-service guarantee flag
- Terms: initial/clean-out, recurring (monthly, bi-monthly, quarterly), re-service or callback, perimeter treatment, bait stations, exclusion (sealing; scorpion sealing), pigeon proofing (solar panel screening), termite WDO/WDI inspection for real-estate sales, and service agreement. (ASSUMED)
- Billing: autopay per service or per month on a contract with an initial fee. Pigeon or solar exclusion is a separately quoted project. Cancellations most often come at renewal or after a missed visit, so automatic "your service is scheduled / done" notices protect retention. (ASSUMED)
- Routing: pest companies set recurring service to "within a window" (any day in the month or quarter), not a fixed weekday. This lets the office re-route by density, which differs from pool's fixed weekday. (INFERRED from the FieldRoutes routing emphasis.)

### Gaps
- NPMA data on retention and frequency mix was not fetched.
- Nevada-specific report formats (whether NDA requires submitting records or only keeping them, and how long) remain unconfirmed.

## 5. Landscaping and lawn care (desert landscaping), irrigation

### Takeaway
Las Vegas landscaping is mostly desert, xeriscape and rock maintenance plus irrigation (drip) upkeep. Water rules drive seasonal clock changes and AB 356 conversions. Software leaders split two ways. LMN and Aspire target commercial and design-build firms with estimating, budgets and job costing. Service Autopilot and Jobber target residential route maintenance.

### Cited Findings
- LMN: estimating, budgeting, job costing, scheduling, time tracking and QuickBooks sync. — [Capterra LMN](https://www.capterra.com/p/142064/LMN/reviews/Capterra___2691146/)
- Aspire: reviewers rate scheduling, quotes and job costing highest, with billing and time tracking in one system and unlimited users (vendor). Aspire targets commercial landscaping. — [Capterra Aspire](https://www.capterra.com/p/161544/Aspire/reviews/7184990/); [Aspire commercial page](https://www.youraspire.com/go/commercial_landscaping_software)

### Inferences
- Residential recurring maintenance in Las Vegas is weekly, biweekly or monthly. It covers blowing rock areas, weeding (pre-emergent and post-emergent sprays need NDA licensing), shrub trimming, palm trimming (seasonal, often summer), checking drip emitters, and changing controller clocks for the SNWA season. Billing is a monthly flat fee. Extras (irrigation repairs, palm skinning, plant replacement, rock top-up) are add-on work orders. (ASSUMED)
- Commercial/HOA contracts: an annual contract billed in 12 equal monthly payments, with "enhancements" quoted separately. Crews of 3 to 6 with a crew lead, and an account manager who walks the property and writes enhancement proposals. (ASSUMED industry norm, reflected in Aspire's commercial focus.)
- Irrigation: work is mostly reactive repair (leaks, broken heads, valve and controller faults), seasonal clock changes, drip conversions for AB 356, and smart-controller installs. A visit record needs zones checked, issues by zone, parts used and before/after photos. SNWA water-waste notices sent to a customer become urgent work orders. (INFERRED)
- Monsoon cleanup (Jul to Sep storms): fallen trees, debris and rock washouts are urgent one-off jobs that need quick quoting and extra crews. (ASSUMED)

## 6. Tree service

### Takeaway
Tree work is mostly estimate-driven projects (an arborist walks the site and quotes removal or pruning), with recurring plant-health-care (PHC) programs added on top. ISA certification is the trust signal, and Arborgold is the leading vertical tool, with tree and plant inventory per property.

### Cited Findings
- ISA Certified Arborist: 3+ years of full-time arboriculture experience, or a degree plus less experience. Renewal every 3 years with 30 CEUs, or by retaking the exam. Reports conflict on the exam format (200 questions in 3.5 h versus 140 in 3 h); the passing score is reported as 76%. — [ISA application](https://www.isa-arbor.com/portals/0/assets/pdf/certification-applications/cert-application-certified-arborist.pdf); [ArboStar explainer](https://arbostar.com/education-hub/what-is-an-isa-certified-arborist); [O*NET](https://onetonline.org/link/certinfo/1752-A)
- ISA Utility Specialist needs 2,000 hours of utility vegetation-management experience in the past 2 years. Municipal Specialist needs 3 years managing urban trees. — [ISA Utility Specialist](https://www.isa-arbor.com/Credentials/Types-of-Credentials/ISA-Certified-Arborist-Utility-Specialist)
- Arborgold is aimed at tree and plant care with recurring services and complex maintenance schedules. It has quotes, scheduling, work orders, billing, mobile access and plant and tree inventory, from about $129/mo flat. — [Software Advice: Arborgold](https://www.softwareadvice.com/arborist/arborgold-profile/); [SourceForge Arborgold vs LMN](https://www.sourceforge.net/software/compare/Arborgold-vs-LMN)

### Inferences
- Workflow (ASSUMED):
  1. A lead comes in.
  2. An arborist estimator visits and builds a map-pinned quote by tree (species, DBH, work, access, equipment such as a crane, bucket or chipper).
  3. The customer signs.
  4. The crew is scheduled by equipment.
  5. Work is done and logged with photos.
  6. The invoice goes out.
  7. A PHC program follows (fertilization, insect/disease treatment, which needs pesticide licensing).
- Las Vegas specifics: palm trimming (summer, after flowering), mesquite and palo verde storm damage in monsoon, and less large-tree removal than wooded regions. (ASSUMED)
- Safety and standards: ANSI Z133 (safety) and ANSI A300 (pruning practice) are what estimates and crews reference (ASSUMED; the search didn't return them).

### Gaps
- No Las Vegas tree-service operators were researched. TCIA accreditation and any Nevada tree-specific licensing were not checked.

## 7. Window, pressure washing, gutter, solar-panel cleaning

### Takeaway
Pricing is per pane (about $4 to $8 nationally; $3 inside and $4 outside in one Las Vegas listing), with hard-water stain removal as a costly add-on. Solar-panel cleaning is a strong Las Vegas line (pure, deionized water; no pressure washer), and it pairs with pigeon proofing. Recurring plans are quarterly, semiannual or annual.

## 8. Snow removal (national only; not relevant to Las Vegas)

### Takeaway
Snow is irrelevant in the Las Vegas valley floor (Mt. Charleston aside). Nationally it runs on three billing models (seasonal flat, per push or event, per-inch tiers) triggered by a contract trigger depth. Recommendation: **keep it as a dormant template, not a Las Vegas build priority.** Kloomie sells nationally, and landscapers in snow states run snow in winter with the same crews and customers.

### Cited Findings

## 9. Cross-trade: scheduling, re-routing, skipped visits, weather days, billing, communication, bottlenecks

### Takeaway
Every one of these trades is "recurring agreement → generated visits on a route → visit record with proof → automatic notice → batch bill." The admin time goes to four things: re-routing after weather or skipped stops, chasing payments, answering "did you come today?", and producing compliance logs. Automating proof-of-service notices, autopay and logs removes most of it.

### Cited Findings
- Pool tools put routes on a map with automatic travel times and drag-and-drop reordering, and send an automatic recap per visit. — [Software Advice AU compare](https://www.softwareadvice.com.au/compare/89281/313522/skimmer/vs/pool-brain); [Skimmer blog](https://getskimmer.com/blog/best-pool-maintenance-software)
- Pest tools turn tech chemical entries into state reports and inventory. — [FieldRoutes blog](https://fieldroutes.com/blog/pest-control-pesticide-usage-tracking-apps); [GorillaDesk](https://gorilladesk.com/features/chemical-tracking-software/)
- Snow tools must keep separate billing paths for seasonal and per-push customers to avoid invoice errors. — [UtilizeCore](https://utilizecore.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-snow-removal-billing-types)

### Inferences (all ASSUMED/INFERRED)
- **Two recurrence models:**
  - A fixed weekday on a fixed route: pool weekly, lawn weekly.
  - A service window: pest monthly or quarterly, window quarterly, PHC by program. The office places these stops by density.

  Software should support both.
- **Skipped-visit handling:** statuses (done, skipped with reason, couldn't access, customer cancelled, weather). Each needs an automatic notice and a rule: reschedule, credit, or no charge.
- **Weather days:** push a whole route a day (rain is rare in Las Vegas, but extreme heat and monsoon wind ground crews). Pest exterior spraying is limited by wind and rain.
- **Gate codes and access notes:** dog, lockbox and gate codes are stored per property and shown only to the assigned tech. They are sensitive data, a fit for Kloomie's sealed fields.
- **Billing:** a monthly flat fee by autopay (pool, landscape, pest monthly), per visit (pest quarterly, window), and per item (chemicals beyond the included amount, parts, pane counts). All are batched at month end, with failed-payment retries and dunning.
- **Lifecycle:** quote → signed agreement (terms, frequency, price, autopay consent) → first or initial visit → recurring visits → seasonal changes (SNWA clock changes; pool summer frequency and chemicals; pest scorpion season roughly Apr to Oct) → upsells from visit findings (a tech flags a "recommended repair" with a photo, which becomes a quote) → price increases at renewal → cancellation (save offer, final invoice, equipment or bait-station pickup).
- **Roles:**
  - owner (sales, pricing)
  - route techs (pool, pest), each with an assigned route
  - crew leads (landscape, tree), running crews of 2 to 6
  - office/dispatcher (routing, calls, billing)
  - account managers or estimators (commercial landscape, tree arborist, window quoting)

**Pool & spa**

- **Billing:** monthly flat fee by autopay, plus chemicals beyond the included amount, plus repair quotes.
- **Biggest automations:** an automatic visit recap email with readings and photos [cited, "digital door hanger"], dose suggestions [cited], a calcium-trend → drain/acid-wash upsell, and a repair flag → quote.

**Pest control**
- **Visit record:** products with EPA registration number, concentration, amount, method, areas and target pests [cited: PestPac fields], applicator and NDA licence number, weather and wind, bait-station checks by number, activity, conducive conditions, photos, signature.

- **Billing:** initial fee plus recurring autopay, plus quoted exclusion and pigeon/solar-proofing projects.
- **Biggest automations:** auto-built compliance logs, the service-done notice (retention), and re-service booking.

**Landscape / lawn (desert) & irrigation**

- **Billing:** 12 equal monthly payments on annual contracts, plus enhancement and repair work orders, batched at month end.
- **Biggest automations:** a seasonal clock-change round, a crew-found issue → enhancement quote, and an SNWA violation notice → urgent work order.

**Tree service**
- **Visit and quote record:** a tree inventory per property (species, size, location pin, condition) [cited: Arborgold inventory], work items per tree, equipment needed, photos, arborist (ISA number).
