# Installer trades in 2026: how they run (Las Vegas first, then national)

Scope: blinds/shades/shutters, flooring, cabinets and countertops, windows and doors, roofing/siding/gutters, fences and decks, security cameras/smart home/AV, EV chargers, closets/garage storage. Researched 2026-11-11.

Labels: **VERIFIED** = stated by the cited source (the URL is given; "vendor claim" means the source is the vendor's own marketing). **ASSUMED/INFERRED** = industry knowledge or reasoning not confirmed by a source found in this pass. Many web results were thin SEO or aggregator pages (permitmint, vanderflip-home, contractorplus, pages.dev and vercel.app sites). They are cited only where nothing better turned up, and flagged "weak source".

---

## 1. How each trade runs: workflow, roles, lifecycle, pricing, payments, software

### Takeaway
All nine trades follow the same core path: lead → site visit/measure → selection/design → quote → deposit → supplier order → receive/stage → install → punch → final payment → warranty. They differ in five places: how measuring is done (tape per opening, laser templating, aerial or photo reports), what the pricing unit is (per opening, per sq ft or sq yd, per roofing "square", per linear foot, per device), whether a permit or inspection sits in the middle, how long the factory lead time is, and how many trips an install takes. Countertops (template → fabricate → install) and roofing (permit, inspections, supplier delivery to the roof) have the most stages. Blinds and closets have the fewest.

### Cited findings

**Blinds / shades / shutters**
- VERIFIED: Budget Blinds (the largest US window-covering franchise) sells its process as three steps (design, measure, install). Products are made to the home's measurements after purchase, and its own staff deliver and install. [Budget Blinds process](https://budgetblinds.com/our-approach/our-process)
- VERIFIED: A Budget Blinds franchise post says lead times went from about 3 weeks to 12–15 weeks during supply-chain delays (older, pandemic-era; date not confirmed). [Budget Blinds Colorado Springs blog](https://budgetblinds.com/greater-colorado-springs-co/community-connection/blog/budget-blinds-delays-backorders--expected-lead-times)
- VERIFIED: Hunter Douglas "Gallery" dealers have premium showrooms and the full product line. [Hunter Douglas FAQ](https://www.hunterdouglas.com/faqs/what-is-a-hunter-douglas-gallery-dealer). Hunter Douglas Installation Services (HDIS) offers dealers measuring and installing through a nationwide network of independent contractors, with published installation standards. [HDIS](https://hdisdealer.hunterdouglas.com/)
- VERIFIED (dealer claim): one Hunter Douglas dealer says shades arrive 3–4 weeks after ordering. [Marigold Home FAQ](https://marigold-home.squarespace.com/faq)
- VERIFIED: Budget Blinds started virtual consultations in 2020. [franchising.com](https://www.franchising.com/news/20200427_budget_blinds_serves_homeowners_with_virtual_consu.html)
- VERIFIED: Blinds software is fragmented. BlindMatrix covers estimating, ordering and scheduling the manufacture of blinds, shutters, curtains and awnings ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/231294/BlindMatrix/)). BlinQ (Australia) captures on-site digital measurements into the customer's job file and converts quote to job in one click ([BlinQ](https://www.blinq.com.au/real-time-window-measurements-blinq-software)). HeadRail is browser-based CRM for window-treatment dealers ([SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/software/product/HeadRail/)). MyBlindCo is an iPad app with CRM and quoting plus a dealer ordering system (2020 trade article) ([WFV](https://issuu.com/wfvision/docs/wfv_m_a_2020/s/11834507)). Quoterite does made-to-measure pricing from supplier price grids ([Quoterite](https://quoterite.com/made-to-measure-software)).
- ASSUMED: Roles at a typical dealer are owner/designer-salesperson (often the same person) who measures at the consult, plus a "check measure" before ordering on big jobs, an office person who places orders on each manufacturer's dealer portal (Hunter Douglas, Norman, Graber/Springs, Alta and others), and 1–3 installers. Pricing is per opening, looked up in the manufacturer's width × height price grid with lift/control, fabric and mount options, and a dealer markup. The deposit is usually 50%, with the balance at install. One job often splits into several manufacturer orders that arrive on different days. A remake (wrong size or damage) is the classic back-order problem.
- ASSUMED: The measurement record per window is room, window name, inside vs outside mount, width × height to 1/8", depth, obstructions, control side, and product/fabric/color. Shutters need extra frame and louver detail.

**Flooring**
- VERIFIED: Measure (RFMS, now sold through Cyncly) and Measure Square create takeoffs, print seam layouts and cut diagrams for installers, and sync with RFMS for invoicing and live product/pricing data. [RFMS Measure](https://www.rfms.com/?p=48827); [Cyncly Measure Desktop](https://cyncly.com/en/products/measure-desktop); [FCNews](https://www.fcnews.net/?p=30913)
- VERIFIED: Measure Square optimizes seam location, layout direction and waste. It keeps different dye lots out of the same room and creates carpet/sheet-vinyl cut sheets with seam diagrams as PDF. [Measure Square](https://measuresquare.com/?p=54). Seams can be edited by hand, but recalculating can wipe custom cuts. [Measure support](https://support.measureflooring.com/hc/en-us/articles/115010684007-View-and-Edit-Seams)
- VERIFIED: Measure Square Mobile lets retail dealers quote on the spot and print the diagram, cut sheet and seam plan. [Floor Trends](https://www.floortrendsmag.com/articles/96767-new-measure-square-mobile-layout-and-estimating-app). Moasure (a motion-based measuring device) integrates with MeasureSquare. [Moasure docs](https://docs.moasure.com/en/integrations/measuresquare)
- ASSUMED: The lifecycle is showroom or shop-at-home visit (Floor Coverings International, Empire Today, 50 Floor), measure, quote, deposit, order. Carpet is ordered by roll width and length; hard surface by carton plus 5–15% waste. Material is received, checked for matching dye lot or run number, and staged. Hard surfaces sit in the home to acclimate (often 48–72 h). Install includes moving furniture, tear-out/disposal and subfloor prep (moisture testing on concrete slabs, which is the norm in Las Vegas). Pricing is per sq ft (hard surface) or sq yd (carpet), with labor, prep, transitions, stairs and trim as separate line items. Installers are often 1099 subcontractors paid per sq ft, so installer pay sheets are a key document.
- VERIFIED (licensing): Nevada C-16 "Finishing floors" with C-16a "Covering floors" excludes ceramic tile and marble. Wood floors fall under C-3b finish carpentry. Tile is C-20. [NAC 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nac/NAC-624.html)

**Cabinets and countertops**
- VERIFIED: Moraware Systemize manages templating, fabrication and installation steps as one job list. AutoSchedule sets tentative later dates with gaps between template, fab and install, so an install is never booked before its fab. It has a shared calendar and route mapping. CounterGo draws, lays out and quotes countertops ("3 minutes", vendor claim) and is listed at $100/user/month. QuickBooks integration. [Moraware Systemize](https://moraware.com/systemize); [Moraware countertop software](https://www.moraware.com/countertop-software/); [Moraware scheduling, Feb 2026](https://www.moraware.com/2026/02/keep-your-shop-organized-with-modern-job-scheduling-and-management/)
- VERIFIED (vendor claim): Night & Day Stone (Las Vegas) uses the same team for templating, fabrication and install, and uses Flexijet laser digital templating. [Night & Day Stone](https://nightanddaystonelv.vercel.app/about) (weak source). Granite Pro (Las Vegas) claims 7–10 business days from template to install for most kitchens. [Slabwise](https://slabwise.com/directory/granite-pro-las-vegas-nv)
- VERIFIED: A 2019 Angi review of a Las Vegas stone company describes prefab granite cut too long or wide. This shows why template accuracy and who templates matter. [Angi](https://angi.com/companylist/us/nv/las-vegas/braza-stone-llc-reviews-7483728.htm)
- ASSUMED: The countertop sequence is: sell/quote from a rough sketch (per sq ft by material group, plus edge profile per linear ft, sink/cooktop cutouts, backsplash, tear-out). The customer picks a slab at the supplier's yard and the slab is put on hold. Template only after cabinets are installed and level, with sinks and faucets on site. Then shop drawing and layout on the slab, customer sign-off on seam placement, CNC/saw fabrication, install, and plumbing reconnect. Remnants are tracked as inventory. Cabinets are measured, designed (2020 Design, Cabinet Vision and similar), ordered (stock about 1–3 weeks; semi-custom/custom 4–12+ weeks), received box by box against the order, with damaged or missing boxes re-ordered. Then install and punch (doors, panels, fillers).
- VERIFIED (licensing): Nevada C-3b finish carpentry covers cabinets, millwork and countertops, except ceramic tile, marble and stone. C-16b covers vinyl/plastic counters. Stone is in C-19/C-20 territory. [NAC 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nac/NAC-624.html)

**Windows and doors**
- VERIFIED: Renewal by Andersen uses a design consultant for the consult and pricing, then a separate project technician who does detailed window and framing measurements before the custom order. Windows are built to within 1/16" of the opening. Installers remove and install, usually in one day for a typical home. [RbA FAQ (2016, older)](https://renewalbyandersen.com/blogs/2016/12/frequently-asked-questions-about-our-window-replacement-process); [RbA install-day checklist](https://www.prod.renewalbyandersen.com/resources/articles/installation-day-checklist)
- VERIFIED (anecdotal reviews): RbA timelines run 3–7 weeks for manufacturing and 4–6 weeks from measure to install, with outliers. One customer was told 16–20 weeks for a front door, double what was promised. Complaints include rescheduled appointments and two-week waits for a service visit. [ConsumerAffairs RbA reviews](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/homeowners/renewal-by-andersen.html?page=6)
- VERIFIED: Hover's measurement reports include openings (windows, doors, sliding doors) from phone photos, plus roofing data even on a siding job. [Hover help](https://help.hover.to/articles/1215021-understanding-hover-s-measurements-pdf); [Pro Remodeler](https://www.proremodeler.com/products/article/55184185/pros-pick-hover)
- ASSUMED: The lifecycle is in-home sale (often one-call-close with financing), then a separate measure-tech visit, then the order (several line items per opening: size, glass package such as Low-E/tint, grids, color, operation). The factory confirms the order and ship date. Partial shipments and remakes (broken glass, wrong size) are common, so one job often needs a second install trip for one or two units. Pricing is per opening by size class and type, plus install type (insert/retrofit vs full-frame or new-construction "stucco cut-back", which is common in Las Vegas stucco homes). Windows cover national franchises (RbA, Window Nation, Pella dealers, Champion) and EPA RRP lead-safe paperwork for pre-1978 homes. Few Las Vegas homes are pre-1978 except older central neighborhoods.
- VERIFIED (licensing): Nevada C-8 glass and glazing; C-14e "Doors and windows" (metal or wood); C-14f storefronts; C-3d overhead (garage) doors. [NAC 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nac/NAC-624.html)

**Roofing / siding / gutters**
- VERIFIED: Roofr turns signed proposals into ABC Supply material orders, checks availability and sends them to the ABC rep for approval (Feb 2025). It pulls SRS Distribution pricing automatically. Roofr measurement reports can be ordered through myABCsupply. [Roofr × ABC](https://roofr.com/product-blog/introducing-material-ordering-with-your-abc-supply-x-roofr-integration); [Roofr × SRS](https://roofr.com/blog/roofr-and-srs-distribution-partner-to-streamline-material-ordering-with-real-time-pricing-integration); [Roofing Contractor](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/101601-roofr-abc-supply-launch-integrated-measurement-reports)
- VERIFIED (vendor): AccuLynx brings ABC Supply's catalog, branch pricing, direct ordering and order-status tracking into the job, and connects with SRS and QXO (QXO bought Beacon in 2025). [AccuLynx ABC](https://acculynx.com/abc-supply-integration/); [AccuLynx material ordering](https://acculynx.com/acculynx-material-ordering)
- CONFLICT: Whether JobNimbus has native supplier ordering is unclear. One comparison says no (manual or Zapier); another 2026 comparison lists ABC, QXO and SRS. Both are weak sources. [ustechautomations](https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/automate-jobnimbus-vs-acculynx-for-roofing-companies-2026); [contractortoolstack](https://contractortoolstack.com/alternatives/acculynx-alternatives/)
- VERIFIED: CompanyCam is mobile-first photo documentation with geotags, timestamps and weather. Hover integrates with CompanyCam and the major roofing CRMs and suppliers (comparison-site claim). [gitnux](https://gitnux.org/best/roof-inspection-report-software/); [contractortoolstack Hover vs Roofr](https://contractortoolstack.com/compare/hover-vs-roofr/)
- VERIFIED: EagleView aerial reports include wall diagrams useful for siding, windows and doors. Hover's report adds a "measurement key". [Valmet-hosted guide (weak, undated)](https://nps-tissueconverting.valmet.com/siding-measurements-101-tape-measure-hover-or-eagleview.html). A competitor reports EagleView at $40–$87 per roof report (self-serving source). [QuoteIQ](https://myquoteiq.com/best-eagleview-alternative-for-roofing-contractors/)
- ASSUMED: Roofs are priced per "square" (100 sq ft) with waste factor, pitch multipliers, ridge/hip/valley/eave/rake linear feet, penetrations and tear-off layers. A good/better/best proposal is standard. Roles are sales rep/estimator (often on commission), production manager, crews (often sub crews paid per square), and office for permits, ordering and insurance supplements. Insurance jobs add adjuster meetings, scope, supplements, depreciation/ACV vs RCV and mortgage-company checks. Las Vegas has few hail claims; more are wind and monsoon. Deliveries go to the roof by boom truck, so a delivery-date confirmation is a key job step. Gutters are priced per linear foot (seamless formed on site).
- VERIFIED (licensing): Nevada C-15 roofing and siding: C-15a roofing, C-15b siding, C-15c insulation, C-15d waterproofing. [NAC 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nac/NAC-624.html)

**Fences and decks**
- VERIFIED: Clark County requires a fence permit for all fences/walls unless exempt. Repairing block walls 6 ft or less and up to 20 linear ft is exempt. Type A = over 6 ft or part of a pool barrier; Type B = fences and retaining walls over 24". [Clark County BPG-232](https://files.clarkcountynv.gov/Building%20&%20Fire%20Prevention/How%20To%20Guides/2022/BPG-232%20Fence%20and%20Block%20Wall%20BPG-051%20Final.pdf); [Clark County fence/wall application BPA-005 (2023)](https://files.clarkcountynv.gov/Building%20&%20Fire%20Prevention/Building%20Submittal/All%20Forms/FenceWall%20Permit%20Application%20BPA-005%2011-28-2023.pdf). There is a county code interpretation for CMU (block) fence and retaining walls. [BD-CI-220](https://files.clarkcountynv.gov/Building%20&%20Fire%20Prevention/Code%20Interpretations/2021/BD-CI-220%20CMU%20Fence%20and%20Retaining%20Walls%20NEW.pdf)
- VERIFIED: The City of Las Vegas has its own fence/wall permit and a multiple-lot wall application. [CLV form](https://files.lasvegasnevada.gov/building-safety/Forms/General/Mutilple-Lot-Wall-Application.pdf). North Las Vegas: walls over 12 ft need a wall-deviation approval from Planning. [NLV checklist](https://cityofnorthlasvegas.com/home/showpublisheddocument/4951/638111695219870000)
- VERIFIED (weak source): HOA architectural review is separate from the permit and often stricter. [siteplancreator](https://www.siteplancreator.com/blog/las-vegas-fence-permit-clark-county-requirements-2026)
- VERIFIED (licensing): C-25 "Fencing and equipping playgrounds"; decks fall under C-3a carpentry (framing, exterior stairs, railings). [NAC 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nac/NAC-624.html)
- ASSUMED: In Las Vegas "fence" usually means CMU block wall (often shared, which needs neighbor sign-off), wrought-iron view fence on block, and vinyl or wood inside yards. Pool barrier rules drive many jobs. Pricing is per linear ft by height and material, plus gates (each), demolition, footing and stucco/cap. Before digging, Nevada 811 must locate utilities (ticket number per job). Decks are less common in the valley than shade structures (alumawood patio covers), which need a permit.

**Security cameras / smart home / AV**
- VERIFIED (licensing): Nevada C-2d "Low voltage systems" covers fiber optics or systems of 91 volts or less, including phone, sound, cable TV and closed-circuit video. C-2c covers fire detection. C-2 electrical covers all C-2 subclasses. [NAC 624.200](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nac/NAC-624.html); [Justia NAC 624.200](https://regulations.justia.com/states/nevada/chapter-624/classifications/section-624-200)
- CONFLICT: Monitored burglar alarms / electronic security may also need a Nevada Private Investigators Licensing Board (PILB) license, according to an alarm company's compliance page, while exam-prep sites say C-2d alone is enough. Verify with NSCB and PILB. [Skyline Security compliance](https://sites.google.com/a/skylinesecurity.com/compliance-and-licensing-department/state-licensing/nevada-nv); [RocketCert](https://rocketcert.com/blog/168-construction/1344-how-to-obtain-a-c-2d-low-voltage-license-in-nevada)
- ASSUMED: Workflow is site survey (camera positions and field of view, network/Wi-Fi, NVR location, cable paths through attic, which is hot in Las Vegas summers so morning installs are preferred), then a design/proposal priced per device (camera, door/window sensor, smart lock, speaker, TV mount) plus labor per drop and hardware. Often an optional monthly monitoring or service plan (recurring revenue). Then install, programming, app handover to the customer, and a commissioning checklist (credentials, camera list with IPs/serials). Equipment comes from distributors (ADI, Snap One) with mostly next-day stock, so back-orders are the exception. Commercial work adds access control, fire alarm (C-2c/C-2g), submittals, and service contracts with response times.

**EV chargers**
- VERIFIED (weak/conflicting): The NV Energy residential Level 2 incentive is reported as the lesser of $500 or 75% of installed cost, for a qualified, connected ENERGY STAR smart charger with managed-charging participation. A managed-charging program reportedly pays $100 per port ($500 income-qualified) plus a $150 annual credit. Amounts conflict across aggregators. Check nvenergy.com. [Clean Energy Authority](https://cleanenergyauthority.com/solar-rebates-and-incentives/nevada/nevada-energy-residential-electric-vehicle-charging-incentive); [NV Energy residential EV handbook (PDF)](https://www.nvenergy.com/publish/content/dam/nvenergy/brochures_arch/cleanenergy/handbooks/residential-electric-vehicle-charging-station-incentives-programs-handbook.pdf); [NV Energy lower-income EV handbook](https://www.nvenergy.com/publish/content/dam/nvenergy/brochures_arch/cleanenergy/handbooks/Lower-Income-EV-Rebate-Handbook.pdf)
- VERIFIED (weak): The federal 30C charger credit was reported to end June 30, 2026. [Qmerit/aggregator cited in search; confirm with IRS](https://qmerit.com/blog/a-comprehensive-guide-to-nevada-ev-tax-credits-rebates-charging-incentives/)
- VERIFIED (weak): Henderson requires electrical permits for Level 2 charger installs. A Level 1 plug-in to an existing outlet needs none. [permitmint Henderson](https://permitmint.com/permits/nevada/henderson/)
- VERIFIED: Qmerit runs a national EV-charger installer network that gets work from automakers and charger brands, with a Nevada page. [Qmerit Nevada](https://qmerit.com/locations/nevada/)
- ASSUMED: The workflow is remote quote from customer photos (panel, meter, garage, distance), which is the Qmerit model, then load calculation (NEC 220), and a panel upgrade or load-management device if needed (NV Energy service upgrade can take weeks). Then permit, install (usually half a day), inspection, rebate paperwork, and charger commissioning/Wi-Fi. Pricing is flat-rate tiers by distance and amperage. The bottleneck is the permit-plus-inspection wait and utility paperwork, not materials. Commercial work adds fleet/multi-port design, load studies, NV Energy commercial incentives and network subscriptions.

**Closets / garage storage**
- ASSUMED (no sources gathered in this pass): California Closets, Closets by Design, Inspired Closets, and garage franchises (GarageExperts, Garage Living, PremierGarage) follow design consult → 3D design in-home → deposit (often 50%) → factory build or in-house cut, about 2–6 weeks → 1-day install. Garage epoxy flooring adds cure time and needs a weather/temperature window; Las Vegas summer garage heat shortens working time. Pricing is per linear foot of system or per design package, plus floor coating per sq ft. Licensing likely falls under C-3a/C-3b in Nevada ([NAC 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nac/NAC-624.html) does not name closets).

### Inferences
- The "deposit → order → receive → install" middle is where every trade loses admin time. An order is placed per supplier, arrives in pieces, and must be matched to a job before the install can be booked. Software that ties purchase orders and line items to the job, and blocks or warns before booking an install while items are missing, solves a problem every trade shares.
- Two-trip jobs (measure-tech visit, remake or partial delivery return trip, template → install) are normal in windows, blinds, countertops and cabinets. The job record needs more than one visit per job and a "remake/backorder" sub-order.

### Gaps
- No primary source found for current (2026) factory lead times by manufacturer (Hunter Douglas, Norman, Andersen, Milgard, cabinet lines); figures above are dealer/review anecdotes.
- No source gathered for closets/garage or for flooring franchise process (Floor Coverings International, Empire Today); described from industry knowledge only.
- Deposit and progress-payment norms are not sourced. Nevada's limits on deposits for residential pools/spas are known, but I did not confirm whether a general residential deposit limit applies to these trades.

## 3. How are supplier orders, lead times, back-orders and partial deliveries tracked against each job?

### Takeaway
Only roofing has strong supplier-to-software ordering (ABC Supply, SRS, QXO/Beacon inside AccuLynx and Roofr). Countertops track slabs and remnants in shop software (Moraware). Flooring ties orders to RFMS. Blinds, windows, cabinets and closets mostly order on each manufacturer's dealer portal and re-key confirmations, ship dates and back-orders by hand into a spreadsheet or CRM notes. ASSUMED for the non-roofing trades.

### Cited findings
- VERIFIED: Roofing: convert proposal → material order → availability check → rep approval (Roofr/ABC). AccuLynx shows order status inside the job. [Roofr](https://roofr.com/product-blog/introducing-material-ordering-with-your-abc-supply-x-roofr-integration); [AccuLynx](https://acculynx.com/abc-supply-integration/)
- VERIFIED: Supplier integrations are often tied to each branch account, so the national partnership does not guarantee a given branch is connected. [contractortoolstack](https://contractortoolstack.com/alternatives/acculynx-alternatives/) (weak source)
- VERIFIED: Flooring: RFMS holds product/pricing and invoicing that Measure syncs to. [Cyncly](https://cyncly.com/en/products/measure-desktop)
- VERIFIED: Countertops: Moraware schedules template → fab → install with automatic gaps. [Moraware](https://moraware.com/systemize)
- VERIFIED: Blinds: BlindMatrix handles ordering and manufacture scheduling. Quoterite offers a dealer ordering portal for makers/wholesalers. [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/231294/BlindMatrix/); [Quoterite](https://quoterite.com/made-to-measure-software)
- VERIFIED: Lead times move a lot (Budget Blinds 3 → 12–15 weeks during disruption; RbA door 16–20 weeks in one review). [Budget Blinds](https://budgetblinds.com/greater-colorado-springs-co/community-connection/blog/budget-blinds-delays-backorders--expected-lead-times); [ConsumerAffairs](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/homeowners/renewal-by-andersen.html?page=6)

### Inferences
- A job's materials model should be: Orders (supplier, PO number, order date, confirmation number, promised ship date) → Lines (item, room/opening, qty, status: ordered / confirmed / backordered / shipped / received / damaged / remake) → Receipts (date, qty received, photo, received-by, where it's staged). The job's install readiness is then computed ("7 of 9 openings received"). Partial install with a return trip for the rest needs a second visit linked to the missing lines.
- Most of the time saved comes from: reading supplier confirmation and shipping emails (outside text treated as data) into the lines; warning when a promised date slips past the scheduled install; and texting the customer automatically when everything has arrived, so they can pick an install date.

### Gaps
- Whether Hunter Douglas, Norman, Andersen, Pella or major cabinet lines offer order-status APIs or EDI to small dealers was not checked.

## 4. How are measurements captured and passed to ordering (per room/opening)?

### Takeaway
Measurement capture splits into four modes. (1) Tape/laser per opening, written on paper or a tablet form (blinds, shutters, windows, closets). (2) Digital takeoff/drawing (flooring with Measure/MeasureSquare and Moasure; closets and cabinets in design software). (3) Laser/digital templating of the actual install surface (countertops, e.g. Flexijet). (4) Aerial or photo-based reports (EagleView, Hover, Roofr; roofing, siding, windows from outside). Only (2)–(4) commonly flow straight into quote and order.

### Cited findings
- VERIFIED: RbA uses a separate project technician for final measurements after the sale, building to within 1/16". [RbA](https://renewalbyandersen.com/blogs/2016/12/frequently-asked-questions-about-our-window-replacement-process)
- VERIFIED: BlinQ captures on-site measurements into the job and converts measure → quote → job. [BlinQ](https://www.blinq.com.au/quote-to-job-conversion-window-furnishing-software)
- VERIFIED: Measure Square produces cut sheets/seam diagrams from the takeoff. Moasure measurements feed into MeasureSquare. [Measure Square](https://measuresquare.com/?p=54); [Moasure](https://docs.moasure.com/en/integrations/measuresquare)
- VERIFIED: Hover measures openings and roof/siding from phone photos and integrates into estimates (ServiceTitan Roofing, Trussi). [ServiceTitan help](https://help.servicetitan.com/roofing/docs/create-estimates-with-hover-in-servicetitan-and-servicetitan-mobile); [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/engineering-and-construction/hover-3d-roof-measurements-now-flow-straight-into-trussi-roofing-estimates.md)
- VERIFIED: Laser templating (Flexijet) is used by a Las Vegas fabricator. [Night & Day Stone](https://nightanddaystonelv.vercel.app/about)

### Inferences
- For Kloomie the core object is a **Measure sheet**: rooms → openings/areas → measurement fields defined by the trade (W×H×D and mount for blinds; W×H, type, glass and install method for windows; sq ft and direction for flooring; linear ft and gates for fences; device list for low-voltage). Each line carries photos and becomes a quote line, then an order line. "Measured by", "check-measured by" and a lock once ordered are what stop costly remakes.
- Importing PDF/CSV reports (EagleView, Hover, Measure Square exports) into the job as attachments plus a few key numbers (squares, pitch, sq ft) is enough. Kloomie need not do takeoffs itself.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed which blinds manufacturers accept electronic order uploads from third-party measuring apps.

## 5. Las Vegas specifics: HOA, monsoon/heat, tile roofs, solar screens, Clark County permits

### Takeaway
Las Vegas installers deal with four jurisdictions (Clark County unincorporated, City of Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas). Most homes are under an HOA that requires architectural approval for exterior changes (walls, roofs, windows, screens, patio covers). Concrete tile roofs dominate, so "reroof" usually means replacing the underlayment (lift-and-relay). Extreme heat drives solar screens, shading and early-morning work. Monsoon wind and rain bring urgent repair calls in July–September.

### Cited findings
- VERIFIED: City of Las Vegas permit guide: replacing a roof covering is exempt from permit, except tile roofing, as long as no structure is repaired and no more than 64 sq ft of sheathing is replaced. So tile reroofs need a permit in the city. [CLV "When do I need a permit"](https://files.lasvegasnevada.gov/building-safety/WHEN-DO-I-NEED-A-PERMIT.pdf)
- VERIFIED (weak sources): The valley has separate permit systems (Clark County / City of Las Vegas / Henderson / North Las Vegas). Reroof permit fee estimates conflict ($100–$400 vs $250–$1,000). [permitmint](https://permitmint.com/permits/nevada/las-vegas/roof/); [propertyzoned](https://www.propertyzoned.com/city/nevada/las-vegas/permits/roofing)
- VERIFIED (forum): Las Vegas tile-roof underlayment lasts about 20–30 years. [BiggerPockets](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/712/topics/977243-tile-roofs-in-vegas-tile-lifespan-vs-underlayment-30lb-felt)
- VERIFIED: Lift-and-relay = remove tiles, replace underlayment and battens, re-lay the original tiles. Some tiles break, so a replacement-tile allowance is needed. It is labor-intensive because every tile is handled twice. [RoofersCoffeeShop](https://www.rooferscoffeeshop.com/post/best-way-repair-tile-roof-system-lift-relay). A tile underlayment product was tested at Nellis AFB (heat performance; article date not checked). [Roofing Contractor](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/86414-tile-underlayment-passes-muster-at-nellis-air-force-base)
- ASSUMED (weak sources only): Contractors say synthetic/high-temperature underlayment is standard locally and that underlayment and deck repair are inspected before tiles go back. Not confirmed in code. [concrete-tile-underlayment.pages.dev (weak)](https://concrete-tile-underlayment.pages.dev/)
- VERIFIED: Clark County fence/block wall permit rules (see section 1). HOA review is separate and often stricter. [Clark County BPG-232](https://files.clarkcountynv.gov/Building%20&%20Fire%20Prevention/How%20To%20Guides/2022/BPG-232%20Fence%20and%20Block%20Wall%20BPG-051%20Final.pdf); [siteplancreator](https://www.siteplancreator.com/blog/las-vegas-fence-permit-clark-county-requirements-2026)
- VERIFIED (weak): Henderson Development Services Center residential permits average 5–7 weeks. Inspections are booked through the Citizen Access portal. [permitmint Henderson](https://permitmint.com/permits/nevada/henderson/)
- VERIFIED: Solar screens are a core product line for local window-covering companies, along with retractable patio screens. [Bloomin' Blinds Henderson](https://bloominblinds.com/henderson); [Fast Track](https://mapquest.com/us/nevada/fast-track-solar-blinds-shutters-345986451)
- VERIFIED: Older (2015) Las Vegas Sun piece on navigating Southern Nevada permitting. [Las Vegas Sun](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2015/jul/13/navigating-permitting-process-southern-nevada/)
- VERIFIED: Nevada licenses most of these trades through the NSCB with classifications under NAC 624 (C-2/C-2d, C-3a/b/d, C-8, C-14e, C-15a–d, C-16a/b, C-19, C-20, C-25). [NAC 624](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nac/NAC-624.html). NSCB says Class C has 42 subclassifications. [NSCB](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/?p=12992)

### Inferences
- ASSUMED: HOA approval is a real stage for exterior jobs (roof tile color/profile, wall height/finish, window frame color, solar screen color, patio covers, exterior cameras sometimes). It typically takes 2–6 weeks with monthly committee meetings. The job needs an "HOA submitted / approved / conditions" step with the approval letter stored. Kloomie could generate the HOA packet (spec sheet, color, site photo, contractor license and insurance).
- ASSUMED: Heat changes scheduling. Roof, attic (low-voltage) and exterior crews start at dawn and stop early in June–September. Adhesives, epoxy and sealants have working-temperature limits. Monsoon storms create a surge of urgent leak calls and tarp jobs that must be fit into a full schedule.
- ASSUMED: Nevada requires a license number on bids and ads (NSCB), and residential contracts above a set amount need specific disclosures. The job's proposal template should carry the license number and limit.
- Solar screens are a natural add-on (cross-sell) for blinds, window and patio companies in the valley.

### Gaps
- Official Clark County reroof underlayment spec and inspection sequence not retrieved (the IRC 2018 with Southern Nevada amendments is the likely base; not confirmed).
- Exact Henderson rules for same-size window replacement permits are unclear (sources conflict).
- NRS 624 residential contract rules (deposit limits, required disclosures, right to cancel) not retrieved in this pass. Important for proposal templates.

## 6. Commercial vs residential differences

### Takeaway
Commercial work in these trades adds bid documents, submittals, certified payroll (on public work), retention (often 5–10%), progress billing on a schedule of values (AIA G702/G703 style), lien waivers, change orders and a GC's schedule. Residential is direct-to-homeowner sales with deposits, financing and HOA approvals. Mostly ASSUMED; few sources gathered.

## 7. Software needs per trade (what a Kloomie job record must hold)

### Takeaway
One shared job structure (customer, site, stages, measure sheet, quote, orders with lines and receipts, visits, documents, payments, warranty) can cover all nine trades. Each trade gets its own measure fields, pricing units, stage list and document set. The automations that save the most admin time across trades: measure → quote → order lines; order tracking with install-readiness; deposit/balance invoices tied to stage moves; permit/HOA/inspection tracking; and customer texts at each stage.

### Cited findings
- VERIFIED: Leading tools prove the value of: proposal → material order (Roofr, AccuLynx) ([Roofr](https://roofr.com/product-blog/introducing-material-ordering-with-your-abc-supply-x-roofr-integration)); auto-scheduling dependent steps (Moraware AutoSchedule) ([Moraware](https://moraware.com/systemize)); measurements straight into quotes (BlinQ, Measure Square) ([BlinQ](https://www.blinq.com.au/real-time-window-measurements-blinq-software); [Measure Square](https://measuresquare.com/?p=54)); geotagged, timestamped photos (CompanyCam) ([gitnux](https://gitnux.org/best/roof-inspection-report-software/)).

### Inferences (per trade; all ASSUMED unless cited above)

**Blinds / shades / shutters / solar screens**
- Record: rooms → openings (mount, W×H to 1/8", depth, control side, product, fabric/color, motorization), photos, measured-by / check-measured-by.
- Stages: Lead → Consult → Quote sent → Sold (deposit) → Check measure → Ordered → Partially received → All received → Install scheduled → Installed → Punch/remake → Paid → Warranty.
- Materials: one order per manufacturer; lines per opening; remake orders linked to the original line.
- Documents: quote with per-window lines, signed order, manufacturer confirmations, install sign-off, warranty card, motor/remote pairing notes.
- Automations: price lookup from W×H grid; "all items in → text customer to book install"; remake tracking; review request after install.

**Flooring**
- Record: rooms with sq ft/sq yd, product per room, direction, transitions, stairs, subfloor/moisture readings, furniture-move and tear-out flags; attach Measure Square/RFMS PDF.
- Stages: Lead → Measure → Quote → Deposit → Ordered → Received (dye lot checked) → Acclimating → Install → Punch → Paid.
- Materials: rolls/cartons by run/dye lot; partial receipt; returns of leftover cartons.
- Documents: seam diagram/cut sheet, installer pay sheet (sq ft × rate), moisture test record, maintenance guide.
- Automations: waste-factor math; installer work order PDF; acclimation timer before install date.

**Cabinets / countertops**
- Record: cabinet list and design file; counter material, slab number/lot, edge, cutouts, sink/faucet/appliance models, seam locations; remnant used.
- Stages (counters): Sold → Slab selected/held → Cabinets ready → Template → Drawing approved by customer → Fabrication → Install → Plumbing reconnect → Paid. (Cabinets: Design → Ordered → Received by box → Installed → Punch.)
- Materials: slab holds at supplier, box-by-box receiving with damage claims, remnant inventory.
- Documents: template file, signed layout/seam approval, damage photos, care guide.
- Automations: Moraware-style dependency scheduling (no install before fab; no template before cabinets are set); "sink/faucet on site?" check before template.

**Windows / doors**
- Record: openings with type, size, glass package, color, grids, install method (retrofit/full-frame), egress or tempered flags, permit number, HOA approval.
- Stages: Lead → Consult → Sold (finance approved) → Measure-tech → HOA/permit → Ordered → Ship date confirmed → Received → Install → Inspection (where required) → Service/remakes → Paid.
- Materials: one factory order with many lines; remake lines; damaged-glass claims.
- Documents: signed contract, finance docs, measure sheet, NFRC labels/energy specs, permit and final, warranty registration.
- Automations: finance status tracking; ship-date slip alerts; second-trip scheduling for leftover units; warranty registration.

**Roofing / siding / gutters**
- Record: measurement report (squares, pitch, linear ft by edge type), roof type (concrete tile, shingle, foam/flat), underlayment spec, tile-breakage allowance, insurance claim info (carrier, claim #, adjuster), permit #, HOA color approval.
- Stages: Lead → Inspection (photos) → Estimate (good/better/best) → Signed → (Insurance: claim → adjuster → scope → supplement) → HOA/permit → Material ordered → Delivery confirmed → Tear-off/dry-in → Underlayment inspection → Tile/finish → Final inspection → Paid → Warranty.
- Materials: supplier order (ABC/SRS/QXO) with delivery date and roof-load; returns credit.
- Documents: inspection photo report, estimate, contract, permit card, inspection results, completion certificate, manufacturer warranty, lien release.
- Automations: report import; proposal → order; weather-aware schedule; monsoon surge triage queue; photo report to the customer and insurer.

**Fences / block walls / decks / patio covers**
- Record: linear ft by segment, height, material, gates, shared-neighbor walls (neighbor consent), pool barrier flag, 811 ticket #, permit type (Clark County Type A/B), HOA approval.
- Stages: Lead → Site measure → Quote → Deposit → HOA → Permit → 811 locate → Materials → Footings/inspection → Build → Final → Paid.
- Documents: site plan (permit), HOA approval letter, neighbor consent, 811 ticket, inspection cards.
- Automations: site-plan sketch from the measure; 811 ticket expiry reminder; HOA packet generator.

**Security cameras / smart home / AV**
- Record: device list per location (camera, sensor, lock, speaker, TV), network info, NVR/hub, serials/MACs, credentials (sealed), monitoring plan.
- Stages: Lead → Site survey → Design/proposal → Deposit → Equipment pulled → Install/pre-wire → Program → Customer handover/training → Paid → Service plan.
- Documents: device/IP schedule, handover checklist, monitoring contract, permits (where required).
- Automations: recurring service/monitoring billing; credentials stored sealed (fits Kloomie's vault/sealed fields); firmware/maintenance reminders.

**EV chargers**
- Record: panel photos, main breaker size, load calc, run distance, charger model, circuit amperage, permit #, inspection result, NV Energy rebate status.
- Stages: Lead (photo intake form) → Remote quote → Approved → Permit → (Panel/utility upgrade) → Install → Inspection → Rebate filed → Paid.
- Documents: load calc, permit, inspection card, rebate application, charger commissioning.
- Automations: photo-intake web form → flat-rate quote; permit/inspection status chasing; rebate paperwork prefilled from the job.

**Closets / garage storage / garage floors**
- Record: wall elevations with lengths/heights, design file/3D renders, finish/color, accessories, floor coating sq ft and system.
- Stages: Lead → Design consult → Design approved → Deposit → Production/order → Received → Install (floors: prep → coat → cure) → Paid.
- Documents: signed design, finish samples, care guide, warranty.
- Automations: design-to-cut list/order; cure-time scheduling; review and referral requests.
